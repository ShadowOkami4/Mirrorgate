# Vanessa Blackwood

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Every royal family has one member everyone underestimates. Sensible historians learn their name first."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **House** [[Blackwood Bloodline]]
> - **Family** Daughter of [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]]; sister of [[Jacob Blackwood]]
> - **Position** Watched very closely by the crown and the royal household

## Role

**Vanessa Blackwood** understands Lustro's court well enough to know when a lie has become policy. She does not approve of her parents or of the crown's denial of [[The Mirrorgate]], but her position leaves her very little room to move. She is more cautious than rebellious and more observant than loud, and more dangerous because of it. When she acts, she acts through hints, go-betweens and carefully placed omissions.

## Wants

- Proof, before she risks anything.
- A way to change the court from inside it.
- To know whether she can trust her brother, or whether they are both watched too closely to speak honestly.

## Secret

> [!secret] What she has
> Vanessa may already be helping someone outside the palace, and she may hold proof that the Mirrorgate exists. The GM decides; a good default is a single letter from a missing courtier, written from somewhere that cannot exist.

## Hooks

- A library book returned to the palace has a margin note in Vanessa's hand, addressed to whoever finds it.
- Vanessa hires the party anonymously to retrieve a confiscated item from the Lustral Office.
