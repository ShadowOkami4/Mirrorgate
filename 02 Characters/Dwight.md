# Dwight

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A medic with a rogue's hands is either a blessing, a crime, or both."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **Known as** The war medic
> - **Companions** [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] and [[Thalrex Silberblick]]
> - **From** The world of Phineas I, where [[Aurora]] is worshipped
> - **Unknown** Full name, homeland, species and fate

## Role

**Dwight** is remembered only in fragments, as a **war medic and rogue**: someone who knew battlefields, wounds, stealth and survival. He was either a healer who moved like a thief, or a thief who learned to keep people alive because the war left him no better choice.

## Possible truths

The GM can pick one, or let the legends disagree:

- A battlefield surgeon who used a rogue's skills to reach the wounded.
- A thief pressed into the war who learned medicine out of necessity.
- A spy whose cover as a medic became his real calling.
- A survivor whose knowledge of death made him invaluable to heroes.

## Hooks

- A bloodstained medical kit bears Dwight's name.
- A coded field journal records Phineas I's travels.
- A healing technique from Dwight's world mends wounds that Kagameru's magic cannot.
- Someone claims Dwight betrayed Phineas; the evidence is incomplete.

> [!player] What players know
> In the old stories of Phineas Phantomhive I, Dwight is the medic with a thief's hands who kept the heroes alive.
