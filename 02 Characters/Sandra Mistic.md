## Overview

Madam Sandra Mistic runs [[To the Eternal Lady]], a secluded inn currently hidden in the [[Forest of Thieves]]. To travellers she is an elegant tiefling innkeeper: warm, composed, observant, and unfailingly courteous. Beneath that living disguise she is an ancient lich.

Sandra is not from [[Kagameru]]. She came from another world with an artifact so dangerous that one mortal lifetime was not enough to guard it. She did not seek lichdom for power or immortality. She accepted undeath as a sentence and a promise: to guard the artifact until her final death. She hates being mistaken for the kind of lich who builds towers from bones; she sees herself as a jailer, an innkeeper, and the last line of defence.

The inn may not be native to Kagameru either. It does not always stand in the same forest, and some stories say it has crossed between worlds. The lively guests inside are magical echoes she made to soften the silence.

> [!warning] The house rule
> Sandra is friendly, but her tavern is protected ground. Violence there can damage the wards around the artifact below. She does not punish spilled ale, raised voices or a desperate swing made in fear. She does not tolerate deliberate, pointless violence.

## When she becomes a boss

Sandra is a **dormant boss**. She only fights when pushed past her limits, and she escalates in steps:

1. **Polite warning.** The room goes quiet, the fire dims, and Sandra asks them to stop.
2. **Unmistakable warning.** Doors lock, glasses crack in unison, and her disguise slips just enough to make the point.
3. **Containment.** Magic separates, restrains, silences or incapacitates the offenders (*Hold Monster*, *Forcecage*, *Silence*, *Power Word Stun*).
4. **Final consequence.** If they keep endangering the inn, she kills them. The dead may sustain her phylactery.

She turns on the party only if they try to break the seals beneath the altar, threaten innocent guests, try to steal the artifact, ignore repeated warnings, or start and continue destructive violence in her tavern without cause. Exposing her identity is **not** enough.

> [!gm]- GM guidance
> Do not punish ordinary roleplay chaos. The rule establishes that the inn is safe ground, not a trap. Give clear warnings before lethal consequences unless the characters commit an obviously monstrous act. Once a fight starts, the statblock's *If Provoked* section holds her legendary and lair actions.

## Appearance

> [!appearance] Living disguise
> - **Look** A poised tiefling woman in refined, practical clothes
> - **Manner** Deliberate movements, a carefully controlled expression, soft laughter
> - **Tells** Does not breathe unless she remembers to; gives off no warmth; rarely eats or drinks in front of guests
> - **Habit** Polishes a glass that was never dirty
> - **Eyes** Always on entrances, shadows and unfamiliar magic

When she drops the disguise, her living features wither into an undead body. Cold light burns in empty eyes and her voice gains a faint echo. She keeps her dignity, but the reveal embarrasses her more than it empowers her.

## Personality

> [!personality] What drives her
> - **Loneliness** Her magical patrons show how much she misses ordinary company
> - **Guilt** She believes her choices helped create the burden she guards
> - **Fear** The artifact behaves in ways she no longer understands
> - **Hope** The adventurers may go where she cannot
> - **Distrust** She keeps dangerous truths divided among as few people as possible
> - **Shame** She does not want kind guests to look at her as a monster

She prefers negotiation to violence and grows quieter, not louder, when angry. She respects careful questions, compassion and restraint; recklessness around unknown magic or innocent guests exhausts her patience.

### Mannerisms and voice

- Addresses strangers formally until they earn her trust.
- Practises breathing, blinking and warming her hands when she thinks no one is watching.
- Pauses before answering questions about her past.
- Never turns her back to the sealed altar; becomes perfectly still when listening to the wards.
- Looks toward an empty chair as though expecting someone there.

> [!quote] In her voice
> *"The inn is safe. I would advise you not to confuse that with harmless."*
>
> *"Undeath is not always an escape from mortality. Sometimes it is merely a promise one has been foolish enough, or desperate enough, to keep forever."*
>
> *"Please do not make me prove what kind of lich I am. I have worked very hard to be an innkeeper tonight."*

## Goals

- **Now:** send the adventurers to the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]; learn why luminous plants are spreading through the forest; find out what happened to [[Maria Rose]]; keep the artifact contained and the inn looking ordinary.
- **Always:** find a way to contain, destroy or return the artifact; learn why it chose the adventurers; keep her secrets out of the wrong hands.

## What she knows

> [!notes] Shares freely
> - Strange planar growth is spreading through the forest.
> - [[Maria Rose]], an old friend investigating it, sent her a plea for help.
> - She cannot leave the inn because of her bond to the artifact.
> - Her letters were enchanted to find people able to answer the call.
> - The [[Temple of the Moon in Water]] is tied to the disturbance and to the [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]] within [[The Mirrorgate]].
> - She is not from Kagameru, and she did not become a lich for power.

> [!gm]- Keeps hidden
> - The artifact reacted to every member of the party.
> - Their gathering resembles part of an old warning or prophecy.
> - Her wards are weakening faster than she admits.
> - The disturbance may not be trying to enter Kagameru, but to retrieve something already here.
> - Her phylactery is sustained by those she judges to have violated the inn beyond forgiveness.
> - She does not know whether the artifact chose the adventurers as protectors, liberators or victims.

## Relationships

- **The adventurers:** necessary but unpredictable. She wants to trust them; their link to the artifact makes her cautious, not hostile.
- **[[Maria Rose]]:** an old friend, now a ghost, investigating the plants near the temple. One of the few people Sandra trusts.
- **[[To the Eternal Lady]]:** home, refuge, prison and part of the containment around the artifact. She suspects the inn is trying to return to a home it has forgotten.
- **The artifact:** not treasure. A world-breaking threat she guards because someone must.
