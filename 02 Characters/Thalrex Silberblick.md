# Thalrex Silberblick

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A dragonborn barbarian with wild magic and glowing blue eyes is the sort of detail legends rarely invent politely."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **Species** Dragonborn
> - **Known as** A barbarian whose magic would not be tamed
> - **Look** Eyes that glow blue, brightest just before his magic erupts
> - **Companions** [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] and [[Dwight]]

## Role

**Thalrex Silberblick** is the powerhouse of the Phineas legends: physically formidable, hard to control, and touched by unstable magic. His magic was never scholarly or disciplined; it came out of him violently and instinctively, above all in battle. He is the perfect foil to Phineas, who could not cast a spark.

## Wild magic

Signs of his magic in the stories:

- Sudden blue light in his eyes.
- Uncontrolled bursts of fire, frost or lightning.
- Spells set off by rage or injury.
- Mirror-like fractures in the air around him.
- Enemies remembering events that did not happen.

## Hooks

- A dragonborn with glowing blue eyes is mistaken for Thalrex's descendant.
- A weapon of Thalrex's releases wild magic when used in anger.
- A Mirrorgate reflection shows Thalrex fighting beside Phineas I.
- A ruin from Phineas's world bears claw marks, scorch marks and blue afterimages that appear a few seconds before the damage does.
