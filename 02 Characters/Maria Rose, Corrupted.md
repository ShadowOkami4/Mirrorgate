# Maria Rose, Corrupted

> [!quote] The red fog, through Maria
> *"Souls of the guided. You who control many bodies. I know what you are."*

> [!danger] Boss of SA-01, Chapter 3
> - **Who** [[Maria Rose]], taken by the red fog in the great hall of the [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]]
> - **When** At the end of [[SA-01 Chapter 3#The Red Fog]]
> - **Difficulty** CR 7 (2,900 XP): about a Moderate encounter for four 5th-level characters (Moderate is 3,000 XP)
> - **Winning** Defeat her, or call the real Maria back three times with *Beneath the Fog*

## What changes

The fog closes around Maria; she screams, and the scream turns into a giggle. She keeps her ghost's body, but the red fog moves her now: crueller, faster and far stronger than she ever was. Her lantern-light turns the colour of a wound.

- **Her face** flickers between Maria and something smiling that is not her.
- **Her voice** is the fog's, until someone reaches her; then, for a moment, it is hers again.
- **The fog** hangs around her in a 10-foot haze that moves with her.

## Running the fight

- The fight is in the great hall, among pillars and the frozen offerings of the night of the ritual.
- Show the *Beneath the Fog* option early: let Maria's real voice break through for a word when a character first speaks her name.
- If the party frees her, the fog pours out of her and sinks into the floor, and Maria collapses, exhausted but herself. Switch back to [[Maria Rose|her own statblock]] for anything that follows.
- If she is destroyed, the fog withdraws laughing. Whether she can return is not yet decided.
