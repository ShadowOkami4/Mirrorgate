> - **Not to be confused with** [[Maria Rose]], Sandra Mistic's ghostly friend

## Role

**Maria Blackwood** is the Queen of Lustro and a Blackwood by birth. To the kingdom she is the house's gracious public face, admired in every portrait and careful in every speech; few people outside the court realise that the crown is truly hers, and that the strictness people blame on Lucien answers to her. She understands that propaganda can be sharper than any blade. Her children oppose her as quietly as they oppose their father.

## Wants

- A spotless public image for the house.
- Good marriages, and loyal heirs.
- Nothing said in public that the Lustral Office would have to unsay.

## Secret

> [!secret] Why she keeps the lie
> Maria keeps the crown's story because she believes it, or because the truth is worse; the GM decides which. Either way, she has noticed that Jacob and Vanessa disapprove, and she has said nothing yet.
