# Maria Blackwood

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"In royal houses, grace is rarely harmless."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **House** [[Blackwood Bloodline]]
> - **Family** Married to [[Lucien Blackwood]]; mother of [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]]
> - **Stands for** The crown's image, and the crown's denial of [[The Mirrorgate]]
> - **Not to be confused with** [[Maria Rose]], Sandra Mistic's ghostly friend

## Role

**Maria Blackwood** is the public face of the royal house: gracious in every portrait, careful in every speech. She understands that propaganda can be sharper than any blade, and she keeps the family's image as carefully as Lucien keeps its order. Her children oppose her as quietly as they oppose their father.

## Wants

- A spotless public image for the house.
- Good marriages, and loyal heirs.
- Nothing said in public that the Lustral Office would have to unsay.

## Secret

> [!secret] Why she keeps the lie
> Maria keeps the crown's story because she believes it, or because the truth is worse; the GM decides which. Either way, she has noticed that Jacob and Vanessa disapprove, and she has said nothing yet.

> [!gm]- Open questions
> - Is Maria a queen, a princess, a duchess, a regent or a diplomat?
> - Is she a Blackwood by birth or by marriage to Lucien?
