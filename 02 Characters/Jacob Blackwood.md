# Jacob Blackwood

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"The quieter branch of a royal family is often quiet because it is listening."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **House** [[Blackwood Bloodline]]
> - **Family** Son of [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]]; brother of [[Vanessa Blackwood]]
> - **Position** Watched very closely by the crown and the royal household

## Role

**Jacob Blackwood** is caught between blood and conscience. He was raised inside the royal machinery of [[Lustro]], but he does not approve of what his parents do, or of the crown's denial of [[The Mirrorgate]]. His disapproval is dangerous: he cannot act without drawing attention, and anyone who approaches him risks becoming part of the surveillance around him.

## Wants

- To stop being complicit in a lie he can see through.
- To keep his sister safe.
- To find out whether he is brave enough to do anything about it.

## Secret

> [!secret] How far he has gone
> Whether Jacob has already contacted [[The Unsilvered]], and how much he knows about the Mirrorgate, is the GM's to decide. A good default: he has passed one message, once, and has been terrified ever since.

## Hooks

- Jacob slips a party member a note at a public ceremony: a time and a place, nothing more.
- A Lustral agent offers the party money to report anyone Jacob speaks to.
