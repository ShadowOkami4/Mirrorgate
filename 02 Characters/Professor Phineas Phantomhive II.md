# Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"If the truth refuses to sit still, one must either chase it or become a librarian. I chose, regrettably, both."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **Known as** The Professor
> - **Family** [[Phantomhive Bloodline]]; nephew of Jürgen and Elisabeth Phantomhive
> - **Work** Chief chronicler of the Encyclopedia Kagami
> - **Began in** [[Hansha]]

## Role

**Professor Phineas Phantomhive II** is a traveller, lecturer and collector of contradictory histories, and the voice behind the Encyclopedia Kagami. His remarks open many notes in this vault: dry commentary, warnings and scholarly corrections that may not be as objective as he believes.

He began his work in [[Hansha]], convinced that the world could be catalogued cleanly. Years of travel through ruins, courts, monster-haunted roads and mirror-touched places cured him of that optimism.

## Wants

- To catalogue the lost histories of [[The Primordial War]] and its [[Scars of the Primordial War|scars]].
- To prove, or disprove, the [[Phantomhive Bloodline#The recurrence problem|recurrence]] of his own family.
- To understand [[The Mirrorgate]], ideally without falling into it.

## Using the Professor

- **Narrator.** His quotes give notes a voice; they are in-world, and not always right.
- **Quest giver.** He pays for lost records, rubbings, eyewitness accounts and anything that contradicts something else.
- **Patron.** He funds expeditions, and expects footnotes.
- **Comic foil.** He is brave in theory and alarmed in practice.
- **Mystery.** He sometimes remembers events he should not.

## Secret

> [!secret] The face in the portrait
> When [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] was the age Phineas II is now, the two looked nearly identical. The Professor knows; he has seen the portrait. He has not decided whether this is family resemblance, Mirrorgate recurrence, or something worse, and he changes the subject when asked.
