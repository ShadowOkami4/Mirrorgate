# Maria Rose

> [!quote] Maria Rose
> *"I came to study a flower. I seem to have found a door instead."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **Known as** The Ghost Lady
> - **True nature** Ghost
> - **Connection** One of the few old friends [[Sandra Mistic]] still trusts
> - **Found in** The great hall of the [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]]
> - **Role in SA-01** The friend whose plea for help starts the adventure

## Role

**Maria Rose** is a ghost who was investigating the glowing plants and the planar disturbance around the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]. Her plea for help reached [[Sandra Mistic]], who cannot leave [[To the Eternal Lady]] and so sent the adventurers in her place.

The party finds her in the great hall of the sanctum, behind the temple's great mirror. She knows the shrine's history, has studied the creatures of the sanctum's outer ring, and senses an evil power gathering there.

## Wants

- To understand why the shrine's two halves are bleeding into each other, and stop it.
- To keep the luminous plants from reaching the Old Road and the villages beyond.
- To see Sandra again, and to tell her something she has put off for a long time.

## Relationship with Sandra

Maria is one of the rare people Sandra trusts with the inn's location and her own nature. Their friendship is old; how old, and how it began, is still open. Sandra's worry for her is personal and urgent, and part of why Sandra trusts the adventurers with the map.

## Secret

> [!secret] What the red fog does
> During the party's conversation with her in the great hall, a red fog rises and corrupts Maria, and the party must fight her. See [[SA-01 Chapter 3#The Red Fog]].

> - Was Maria already a ghost when she began the investigation, did she die during it, or is she ghostlike because the Mirrorgate displaced her from her time and place?
> - Her ancestry, homeland, age, appearance and former occupation.
> - How she and Sandra met, and whether Sandra knows the truth of her condition.
