# Phineas Phantomhive I

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A name may cross worlds more easily than a man. Unfortunately, in this case, both appear to have done so."*

> [!personality] At a glance
> - **Known as** The Original Phineas
> - **From** Another world, whose main faith is [[Aurora]]
> - **Companions** [[Dwight]] and [[Thalrex Silberblick]]
> - **Famous for** Being a hero who could not use magic at all

## Role

**Phineas Phantomhive I** is the strangest figure in the [[Phantomhive Bloodline]]. He was not born in [[Kagameru]] but in another world, where the dead rarely return and a hero with no magic was remembered as one of the greatest of his age. How he, or his name, came to Kagameru is unknown.

## A hero without magic

Every legend agrees: Phineas could not use magic. It was not a lack of talent or training; something in him simply could not hold it. His spells came to nothing, and magic items that answered anyone else lay dead in his hands. He fought with nerve, cunning, stubbornness and two companions who never left his side. [[Thalrex Silberblick]] could barely control the magic pouring out of him, Phineas could not summon a spark, and the stories insist it was usually Phineas who knew what to do.

## The family he kept

A dragon destroyed Phineas's home and everyone he loved. In his world, resurrection was possible but difficult, costly and rare, far beyond an ordinary family. So Phineas kept his family another way: after the attack, their voices began to speak through him, exactly as he remembered them. The best known are his uncle **Jürgen** and his aunt **Elisabeth Phantomhive**. At times one of them would take his place entirely, with their voice, their habits and the advice or scolding they would have given in life. His companions learned to recognise who was speaking and answer them by name.

> [!important] Not the later Jürgen and Elisabeth
> The Jürgen and Elisabeth who lived in Phineas were his memories of his uncle and aunt. The Jürgen and Elisabeth of later family records are different people, and yet they too are the uncle and aunt of a Phineas. See [[Phantomhive Bloodline#The recurrence problem]].

## The world he left behind

What is known of his world comes from his legends and from one traveller who crossed into it through [[The Mirrorgate]] and, against all reason, came back.

- **Faith.** Its main religion is [[Aurora]], a genderless deity of life. It is **not** the main religion of Kagameru.
- **Death.** Resurrection exists but is rare; for most people, death is final.
- **Syrid.** The traveller brought back word of a crystal-like substance called *Syrid*. Nothing more is known.

## Secret

> [!secret] The resemblance
> At the age Phineas II is now, Phineas I looked nearly identical to him. Inheritance, Mirrorgate distortion, a repeating soul, deliberate imitation, or something stranger: no answer is safe yet.

## Hooks

- A portrait of Phineas I is mistaken for Phineas II.
- An artifact of Aurora reacts to the Phantomhive name.
- A journal of Phineas I changes handwriting mid-page as Jürgen or Elisabeth takes over.
- A mirror shows Phineas's home as it was before the dragon came.
- A merchant offers a shard of Syrid, brought back through the Mirrorgate. It may even be real.
