# Adventures & Fiction

> *“The difference between history and adventure is usually whether anyone survived long enough to organize the notes.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

This section gathers playable adventures, campaign premises, fiction fragments, session material, and narrative hooks set within the Mirrored Realms.

## Release Collections

- [[One-Shots]] — self-contained adventures and episodic standalone tales
- [[Multishots]] — short adventures designed for more than one session
- [[Campaigns]] — long-form frameworks for extended play

## Currently in Development

- [[The Pack in the Twilight]] — short adventure, 5%
- [[Shattered Hope]] — main campaign framework, 1%

For the complete production overview, see the [[Roadmap]].

## Common Adventure Themes

- relics that choose their bearers
- ruins that remember the wrong century
- monsters born from broken reflections
- noble houses with missing records
- travelers caught between local danger and cosmic consequence
