# To the Eternal Lady

> *“Every lonely road eventually invents an inn. This one, regrettably, may have invented itself.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**To the Eternal Lady**, often called **the Eternal Lady Inn** in travelers' tales, is the secluded inn operated by [[Sandra Mistic]]. It currently stands in the [[Forest of Thieves]], but calling it a local inn is misleading.

The Eternal Lady is not truly stationary.

Some travelers claim the inn changes forests. Others insist it has changed worlds. The most unsettling accounts suggest that the inn is not wandering at random: it is trying to return to the world it belongs to, but has forgotten where that world is.

To travelers, it appears as a warm refuge in a dangerous wood. To those who look closer, it is a protected threshold, a prison, a living wound in geography, and a vigil disguised as hospitality.

## Current Adventure Role

- meeting place where the party first encounters Sandra Mistic
- safe-looking refuge after the forest ambush
- magical structure containing illusions, echoes, and concealed wards
- entrance to the chamber where Sandra reveals her true nature
- wandering location that may connect Zerkalo to other worlds
- clue that Sandra, the artifact, and the inn may all be displaced from where they belong

## The Wandering Inn

The Eternal Lady does not remain in one place forever. It appears where roads grow lonely, where forests become difficult to leave, and where the boundary between worlds has softened.

Its movement is subtle. Few people witness it vanish. Instead, stories emerge:

- A hunter enters the inn in one forest and leaves into another.
- A merchant finds the same tavern in two countries several years apart.
- A child swears the moon outside the windows had the wrong color.
- A road that led to the inn yesterday ends at an ordinary clearing today.
- A traveler recognizes the signboard from a world they have never visited.

Whether the inn chooses its destinations or is dragged between them remains unknown.

## Forgotten Home

The strongest rumor says the inn is searching for its original world. Something within it remembers that it belongs elsewhere, but not where “elsewhere” is.

This forgotten longing may be connected to [[Sandra Mistic]], the artifact beneath the inn, or the magic that binds them together. Sandra does not freely discuss the matter. When pressed, she may admit that the inn sometimes dreams of roads she has never walked.

> [!question] Open Mystery
> Did Sandra bring the inn with her, did the inn bring Sandra, or did the artifact bind them both into the same wandering fate?

## First Impressions

The inn should feel welcoming enough to lower suspicion, but precise enough to become unsettling.

- The fire is warm, but the room has no smoke.
- Patrons laugh and drink, but their conversations repeat.
- The floor is clean despite the mud outside.
- Sandra knows exactly when the party arrives.
- Windows sometimes show treelines that do not match the forest outside the front door.
- Old travel scratches on the tables name roads, kingdoms, and worlds that no local has heard of.

## House Rule

Sandra works hard to make the tavern feel like a place of warmth and safety. She forgives fear, confusion, and minor disorder, but she does not tolerate senseless violence within her walls.

If guests begin a destructive fight without cause and refuse to stop after clear warnings, Sandra treats them as threats to the sanctuary. At that point, the inn stops being cozy scenery and becomes the lair of a lich who has very practical uses for corpses and souls.

## Useful Checks

- **DC 12 Investigation:** Notice repeated patterns among the patrons.
- **DC 13 Insight:** Realize the inn's guests are emotional echoes rather than ordinary travelers.
- **DC 14 Arcana:** Detect layered illusion and abjuration magic woven through the building.
- **DC 15 Perception:** Notice that Sandra does not breathe.
- **DC 15 Survival:** Realize the inn's position does not match the surrounding paths; the road seems to have adjusted itself around the building.
- **DC 16 Arcana:** Detect planar displacement woven into the foundations. The inn has been moved across great distances, possibly across worlds.
- **DC 17 History:** Recognize a carved place-name that does not belong to any known region of Zerkalo.
