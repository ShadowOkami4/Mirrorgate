AI-generated or AI-assisted images are currently used as provisional visual aids. Artists are warmly invited to contribute original artwork or propose paid commissions. Payment may be requested and discussed, but cannot be promised; scope, rights, attribution, and compensation must be agreed before work begins.

Images imported into Atlas VTT are stored in `atlas-vtt/assets/`. The starter tokens supplied by the Atlas VTT plugin are third-party artwork; they are not project material and are not published with the repository. See the [[Atlas VTT Guide]].

When adding an asset, record its creator, source, license or permission, related notes, and whether it is final or provisional. Do not assume that the repository's content license applies to third-party artwork unless the asset is explicitly identified as covered.
