# Soundtrack

Every important place and character in the Mirrored Realms has a music recommendation for the table.

> [!important] Recommendations Only
> The tracks on this page are **recommendations**. No music is included in the vault or the repository. Every named track belongs to its composer and rights holders, recorded in the *Source* column. Listen to it through an official source.
>
> Self-made remixes are planned for every track. Remixes of copyrighted music will only be published where that is permitted, and they are **not** covered by the project's CC BY 4.0 licence.

## How Recommendations Work

- **Specific track:** a named piece from a game, film, or other work, listed with its franchise, composer, and rights holder.
- **Generic:** a mood description rather than a named track. Any music that fits the description works.
- **Remix:** the status of the planned self-made remix.

## Adding a Recommendation

- Add every new important place or character to this page.
- For a specific track, record its title, franchise or work, composer, and rights holder, for example: *Track Title* — Game Title (Composer, © Rights Holder).
- Many game tracks exist in several versions or parts. Name the part in the *Recommendation* column, for example: *Resurrections* — part: **Blocks Active**.
- Set *Remix* to `planned`, `in progress`, or `released`.
