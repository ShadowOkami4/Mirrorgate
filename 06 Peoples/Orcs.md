# Orcs

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"The dullest scholars call orcs violent and stop writing. Better scholars ask who taught them to survive."*

## Overview

The orcs of [[Kagameru]] are not one culture, nation or story. Their communities vary widely across the world, shaped by homeland, history, faith, trade, war and alliance: clans, city neighbourhoods, warbands and settled farming families all call themselves orcs, and many would not recognise each other's customs.

## Culture

- **Many ways of living.** Orcs live as clans in harsh country, as families in human cities, as rail workers and miners, and as warbands where the land gives them no other choice.
- **Honour, differently.** Orcish honour traditions differ sharply from region to region: in one, a debt is paid in labour; in another, in a story told about you at the fire.
- **Borderlands.** Many orc communities live in the borderlands, harsh terrain and contested territory that kingdoms want but will not settle, and they know those lands better than anyone.
- **A reputation they did not choose.** In [[Lustro]], official histories have not been kind to orcs. Many orcs have good reason to be suspicious of approved histories.

## Playing one

- A miner from [[Cinderfold]] who saw something in a new shaft that the company would rather nobody mention.
- A borderland guide who knows the paths around [[The Watchful Gate]].
- A clan's storyteller, sent out to collect a story worth telling.
