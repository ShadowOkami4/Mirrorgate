# Dragonborn

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Every dragonborn family has a dragon in its story. Most of them would prefer it stayed there."*

## Overview

Dragonborn carry the look and breath of dragons, and every family tells a different story of how that came to be: descent from a dragon, a pact with one, or a curse that went better than intended.

## Culture

- **Southern clans.** Many dragonborn families live in the stone cities of [[Tezcatl]], where the volcanic heat suits them and the great temples keep records of their lineages.
- **Family and breath.** A dragonborn's ancestry decides the colour of their scales and the element of their breath, and families are fiercely proud of it.
- **Outsiders' stories.** In the north, many people know dragonborn only from the legends of [[Thalrex Silberblick]], which does not help their reputation for calm.

## Playing one

- A temple-record keeper from Tezcatl whose lineage book has a page torn out.
- A rail guard in Lustro, tired of being asked about Thalrex.
