# Elves

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Long lives do not make a people patient. They simply give impatience more time to become tradition."*

## Overview

The elves of [[Kagameru]] are an ancient and diverse people whose histories often reach further back than the written records of younger kingdoms. There is no single elven nation: there are courts that are fading, communities that are thriving, and families who remember things everyone else has agreed to forget.

## Culture

- **Long memory, contested past.** An elf may have heard the story of a war from someone who fought in it, and still disagree with every book written about it. Elven accounts of [[The Primordial War]] are among the oldest, and among the most argued over.
- **Old forests and star-lore.** Many elven communities live among the old forests of [[Drychwen]] and the ancient groves of [[Veidra]], where the earliest magical traditions of the world are said to have begun.
- **Preservation.** Elven cultures are shaped by patience, pride and grief. They keep things: songs, groves, grudges and libraries.
- **Many cultures.** A court elf of Drychwen and a grove-keeper of Veidra may have little in common beyond their long lives.

## Playing one

- An archivist who remembers a version of history the [[Lustral Office]] has erased.
- A grove-keeper whose forest's reflection in [[The Mirrorgate]] has begun to grow wrong.
- The last courtier of a fading court, sent to find out what the younger kingdoms have become.

> [!rule] Rules
> Use the SRD 5.2.1 **Elf** species. Its lineages fit Kagameru's elves as written; which lineage belongs to which homeland is up to the GM.

> [!gm]- Open questions
> - Which elven realms or courts exist, and where?
> - What do the elves remember about the Mirrorgate that humans do not?

> [!player] What players know
> Elves are a long-lived, ancient people, most at home in the old forests of Drychwen and the groves of Veidra. They remember a great deal, and argue about all of it.
