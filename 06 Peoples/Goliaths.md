# Goliaths

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A goliath measures a mountain by how long it takes to climb, and a person by whether they kept up."*

## Overview

Goliaths are towering people with a touch of the giants in their blood: strong, competitive and at home in high, harsh places.

## Culture

- **The cold north.** Goliath holds lie in the mountains and cold coasts of [[Skuggsjá]], where hard winters reward strength and fair dealing.
- **Giant ancestry.** Each goliath carries the mark of a giant kind (frost, fire, hill, stone, cloud or storm) and the gifts that come with it.
- **Fair contest.** Goliath communities settle a great deal by contest, and they keep score.

## Playing one

- A Skuggsjá guide who came south for a contest and stayed for the trains.
- A goliath whose giant ancestry shows a kind of giant nobody has a name for.
