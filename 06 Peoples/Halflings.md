# Halflings

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Ask a halfling for directions and you will get directions, a snack, and a warning about the road you did not ask about."*

## Overview

Halflings are small, nimble and lucky, with a talent for comfort and for slipping out of trouble.

## Culture

- **Farm country.** Many halflings live in the farming villages of [[Specula]], along roads like [[The Old Road]], and in the market towns between them.
- **Inns and roads.** Halfling families run a good share of Specula's roadside inns and know every rumour that passes through.
- **Lucky.** Halflings have a reputation for surviving things that should not be survivable, and do not like to talk about it.

## Playing one

- A halfling from [[Glasscombe]] who went into the Forest of Thieves after a lost goat and came back with a glowing flower.
- An innkeeper's child who has read every letter guests left behind.
