# Dwarves

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A dwarf does not merely build a hall. A dwarf argues with the mountain until the mountain agrees to become architecture."*

## Overview

The dwarves of [[Kagameru]] are known in many regions for endurance, craft, memory, and a practical suspicion of anything called "ancient power" by someone holding a shovel. They live in mountain holds and fortified settlements, and their deep roads run where no railway ever could.

## Culture

- **Holds and deep roads.** Dwarven holds are fortresses as much as homes, linked by tunnels older than most surface kingdoms.
- **Stone, metal and oath.** Dwarven craft traditions are bound up with promises: a smith's mark is a vow, and a broken tool is a broken word.
- **Clan memory.** Clans keep written records of every obligation, owed and owing, sometimes for centuries.
- **Relics, carefully.** Dwarves dig into ruins that refuse to age correctly. They have learned, at great cost, to handle such relics practically: sealed, labelled, and never alone.
- **The unmirrored monuments.** Dwarven monuments deep in caves, where no water has ever stood, appear in [[The Mirrorgate]] in near-perfect detail because generations of eyes have looked at them. Some clans consider this an honour; others consider it a security problem.

## Playing one

- A clan archivist who found an obligation in the records owed by a family that no longer exists.
- A delver whose hold broke into a ruin where time runs wrong.
- A smith who forged a mirror and has regretted it since.
