# Gnomes

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Hand a gnome a broken clock and you will get back a working clock, two improvements, and a lecture."*

## Overview

Gnomes are small, curious and inventive, with long lives and a habit of taking things apart to see why they work.

## Culture

- **Hansha's workshops.** Many gnomes live in [[Hansha]], among its engineers, printers and copying-houses; some of the newer steam lines were their idea.
- **Tinkers and illusionists.** Gnomish traditions run from clockwork to illusion magic, often in the same family.
- **The ancient lines.** Gnomish engineers are among the few who study the ancient railway lines seriously, and the most frustrated by them.

## Playing one

- A Hansha engineer who measured an ancient line and got a different length every day.
- An illusionist who studies mirrors professionally, and has started seeing things in them.
