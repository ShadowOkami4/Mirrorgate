# Peoples

Species and cultures of Kagameru.

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"To study a people by their appearance alone is to judge a library by the color of its door."*

The peoples of [[Kagameru]] are as varied as its lands: ancient lineages beside young civilisations, wandering clans crossing the borders of settled kingdoms, and neighbouring villages a few miles apart that keep completely different customs.

**Species and culture are not the same.** Members of one species may belong to rival nations, speak different languages and worship different gods. One community may hold people of many species, joined by a shared homeland and history. These notes describe common traditions, not rules every individual follows; adventurers, in particular, have a habit of defying tidy categories.
