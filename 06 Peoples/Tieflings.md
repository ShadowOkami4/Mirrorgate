# Tieflings

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A tiefling's horns tell you about their ancestors. Their manners tell you about them."*

## Overview

Tieflings carry the mark of fiendish ancestry: horns, unusual eyes and skin, and a legacy of infernal, abyssal or chthonic power.

## Culture

- **The shadowed coasts.** Many tieflings live in [[Scáthan]], where planar distortion is part of everyday life and nobody stares at horns.
- **Legacies.** Each tiefling's fiendish legacy (abyssal, chthonic or infernal) shapes their magic, not their character.
- **Not all from Kagameru.** Some tieflings, like [[Sandra Mistic]], came from other worlds entirely; locals rarely notice the difference.

## Playing one

- A Scáthan sailor whose ship's mirror shows a coast that is not on any chart.
- A tiefling in Lustro who keeps their papers very carefully in order.
