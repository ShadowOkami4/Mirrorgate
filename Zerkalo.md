# Zerkalo

> *“Every world believes itself ordinary until one reads its footnotes.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Zerkalo** is the central world of the Mirrored Realms: a land of old scars, unfinished histories, contested relics, and reflections that do not always obey their owners.

Its peoples build kingdoms, roads, temples, academies, inns, and border forts atop the remains of powers far older than their maps. Beneath those foundations lie ruins from the age of [[The Primordial War]], wounds where time and space still bleed, and doors into places no sensible traveler would call safe.

## Defining Features

- The world was shaped by the legendary [[The Primordial War]].
- The [[Scars of the Primordial War]] remain one of its greatest sources of wonder, danger, and contradiction.
- The present age is increasingly known as [[The Shattered Hope Era]].
- Certain mirrors and planar wounds connect Zerkalo to [[The Mirrorgate]].
- Time, space, reflection, and memory are central forces in its oldest mysteries.
- Its peoples and cultures are recorded in [[Species & Cultures]].
- Its lands and political powers are gathered under [[Geography & Politics]].

## Current Understanding

Zerkalo is not a fully settled or fully understood world. Many of its maps are incomplete, many of its histories contradict each other, and many of its ancient sites have been sealed for excellent reasons that modern explorers are determined to ignore.

## Campaign Use

Zerkalo is designed to support campaigns about:

- relic-hunting and ruin exploration
- political conflict over ancient war-scars
- divine mysteries and lost gods
- mirror-world travel, unstable realities, and broken chronology
- noble houses with dangerous histories
- ordinary people trying to survive extraordinary forces
