# Soul Shard

*Small Aberration, Chaotic Neutral*

> A flickering fragment of a former self: barely sentient, painfully lonely, and hungry for contact.

---

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 13 (3d6 + 3)  
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 (-3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) | 11 (+0) | 13 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Cha +3  
**Skills** Stealth +4  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic, psychic; bludgeoning from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** understands no language  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  

---

## Traits

**Ghostly Presence.** The soul shard can move through other creatures' spaces. It cannot willingly end its movement in a space occupied by more than one other soul shard.

**Soul Echo.** Whenever the soul shard is within 10 feet of a dying creature, it gains a +1 bonus to its next attack roll, to a maximum of +3. This bonus lasts for 1 minute.

## Actions

**Spirit Sting.** *Melee Spell Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 4 (1d4 + 2) psychic damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

## Reactions

**Shard Flicker (1/Day).** When the soul shard is hit by an attack, it vanishes in a flash and reappears in an unoccupied space within 15 feet. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. Each creature within 5 feet of the space it left must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.
