# Shard Monsters

> *“A broken mirror does not stop reflecting. It merely becomes less polite about what it shows.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Shard Monsters** are creatures warped by exposure to [[The Mirrorgate]]. Some were once living beings. Others may be emotions, memories, or reflections that learned to move without an owner.

They are most often found near unstable mirrors, glasslike planar wounds, ruins touched by impossible time, or places where identity has been magically damaged.

## Known Shard Monsters

| Creature | Role | Challenge |
|---|---:|---:|
| [[Soul Shard]] | fragile psychic fragment | CR 1/2 |
| [[Shard Zombie]] | broken corpse animated by glasslike force | CR 1/2 |
| [[Shattering Soul]] | stronger identity-hunting aberration | CR 2 |

## Common Signs

- bodies held together by reflected light or glasslike shards
- voices that mimic the last person they harmed
- memories leaking into nearby dreams
- mirrors fogging, cracking, or showing the creature before it appears
- attacks that damage identity, memory, or perception

## Running Them

Shard Monsters work best when they feel tragic as well as dangerous. They are not ordinary beasts; they are what remains when a person, corpse, or memory is broken by the wrong reflection.
