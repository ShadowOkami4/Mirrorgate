# Shattering Soul

*Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil*

> A fragmented existence that remembers being whole and hates every living creature that still is.

---

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +3, Cha +4  
**Skills** Deception +4, Stealth +4  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic, psychic  
**Condition Immunities** frightened  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11  
**Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but cannot speak  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  

---

## Traits

**Shard Body.** The first time the shattering soul drops to 0 hit points, it does not immediately vanish. Instead, it leaves behind a soul shard until the start of its next turn.

**Disturbed Reflection (1/Day).** As a bonus action, the shattering soul creates an unstable double of one creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or believe it is fighting itself until the end of its next turn. While affected, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than the shattering soul and cannot willingly move farther away from it.

## Actions

**Soul Rip.** *Melee Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 10 (2d6 + 3) psychic damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or suffer a brief memory fracture until the end of its next turn. The GM chooses the effect, such as forgetting a prepared response, failing to recognize an ally, losing access to one language, or being unable to recall why it entered the room.

**Shard Touch.** Once per round, immediately after the shattering soul hits with Soul Rip, the target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or take 2 (1d4) necrotic damage and have disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes against a spell before the end of its next turn.

## Death Effect: Soul Shard

When the shattering soul dies, it leaves behind a Tiny soul shard with 1 hit point. At the start of the shattering soul's next turn, the shard explodes in a 10-foot radius. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be confused until the end of its next turn.

After the explosion, the shard disappears.
