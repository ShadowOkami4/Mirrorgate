# To the Eternal Lady

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Every lonely road eventually invents an inn. This one, regrettably, may have invented itself."*

> [!location] At a glance
> - **Kind** Inn that does not stay where it is
> - **Keeper** [[Sandra Mistic]]
> - **Currently** In a sheltered hollow about 3 km (2 miles) off [[The Old Road]], reached by an unmarked side path, 10–20 km inside the southern edge of the [[Forest of Thieves]]
> - **Known as** The Eternal Lady Inn, in travellers' tales

## Overview

**To the Eternal Lady** is the secluded inn kept by [[Sandra Mistic]]. To travellers it is a warm refuge in a dangerous wood: a fire, a clean table, good wine and an elegant tiefling who remembers everyone's favourite drink. Calling it a local inn is misleading. The Eternal Lady does not stay in one place.

## The wandering inn

The inn appears where roads grow lonely, where forests become hard to leave, and where the boundary between worlds has worn thin. Few people ever see it vanish; instead, stories pile up:

- A hunter enters the inn in one forest and leaves into another.
- A merchant finds the same tavern in two countries, several years apart.
- A child swears the moon outside the windows had the wrong colour.
- A road that led to the inn yesterday ends at an ordinary clearing today.
- A traveller recognises the signboard from a world they have never visited.

The strongest rumour says the inn is trying to go home. Something in it remembers that it belongs to another world, but not which one. Sandra rarely discusses it; pressed, she admits that the inn sometimes dreams of roads she has never walked.

> [!question] Open mystery
> Did Sandra bring the inn with her, did the inn bring Sandra, or did something else bind them both into the same wandering fate?

## Inside

- **The common room.** A long room with a low ceiling, a great hearth and a dozen tables. The patrons laugh and drink, but they are magical echoes Sandra made to soften the silence; their conversations repeat.
- **The bar.** Sandra polishes a glass that was never dirty. Behind her hangs a mirror that shows the room a little emptier than it is.
- **The guest rooms.** Upstairs, always exactly as many as there are guests.
- **The altar room.** Behind a locked door at the back, a stone altar seals the hidden vault where Sandra guards the artifact. Its wards are part of the building.

> [!gm]- First impressions
> The inn should feel welcoming enough to lower suspicion and precise enough to become unsettling.
> - The fire is warm, but there is no smoke.
> - The floor is clean despite the mud outside.
> - Sandra knows exactly when the party arrives.
> - Windows sometimes show tree lines that do not match the forest outside the front door.
> - Old scratches on the tables name roads, kingdoms and worlds no local has heard of.

> [!warning] The house rule
> Sandra forgives fear, confusion and minor disorder, but not senseless violence. Violence in the common room strains the wards over the vault. If guests start a destructive fight without cause and ignore clear warnings, the inn stops being cosy scenery and becomes the lair of a lich. See [[Sandra Mistic#When she becomes a boss]].

> [!rule] Useful checks
> - **Intelligence (Investigation) DC 12:** notice repeated patterns among the patrons.
> - **Wisdom (Insight) DC 13:** realise the guests are emotional echoes, not ordinary travellers.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 14:** detect layered illusion and abjuration magic woven through the building.
> - **Wisdom (Perception) DC 15:** notice that Sandra does not breathe.
> - **Wisdom (Survival) DC 15:** realise the paths do not fit the building; the road seems to have adjusted itself around the inn.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 16:** detect planar displacement in the foundations. The inn has been moved across great distances, possibly across worlds.
> - **Intelligence (History) DC 17:** recognise a carved place name that belongs to no known region of Kagameru.

> [!gm]- Adventure role (SA-01)
> - Where the party first meets Sandra, after the forest ambush
> - A safe-looking refuge full of illusions, echoes and concealed wards
> - The room where Sandra reveals her true nature
> - A wandering place that may connect Kagameru to other worlds, and a clue that Sandra, the artifact and the inn are all displaced

## Hooks

- A guest at the next table insists they checked in yesterday; the ledger says forty years ago.
- The inn's signboard has started showing a second name, painted over the first.
- One echo in the common room stops repeating and asks the party for help.

> [!player] What players know
> To the Eternal Lady is a warm inn hidden deep in the Forest of Thieves, a few miles off the Old Road down an unmarked path. Its keeper, Madam Sandra Mistic, is a courteous tiefling with one firm rule: no fighting in her house. Travellers' tales claim the inn has been seen in other forests, too.
