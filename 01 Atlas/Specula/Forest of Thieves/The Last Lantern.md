# The Last Lantern

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Every traveller who enters the Forest of Thieves stops at the Last Lantern. The landlord counts them in. He tries not to count them out."*

> [!location] At a glance
> - **Kind** Roadside tavern
> - **Where** On [[The Old Road]], a short walk past [[Glasscombe]], where the road enters the trees
> - **Known for** The lantern over the door that burns day and night

## Overview

**The Last Lantern** is a lonely tavern where the Old Road enters the [[Forest of Thieves]]. Travellers call its lantern the last honest light before the forest. The thieves of the forest leave the tavern alone, by an old and unspoken agreement.

## Inside

- **The ledger.** The landlord writes down everyone who goes into the forest, and crosses them out when they come back. Most pages are not crossed out.
- **Guides for hire.** Locals offer to lead travellers in, but only as far as the first glowing plants.
- **Letters.** A bundle of letters waits behind the bar, addressed to adventurers who have not arrived yet.

## Hooks

- The ledger shows a name that went into the forest forty years ago and came back last week.
- One of the waiting letters is addressed to a member of the party, in Sandra Mistic's handwriting.

> [!player] What players know
> The Last Lantern is the last tavern before the Forest of Thieves. You can hire a guide there, hear the latest rumours about the road, and sign the landlord's ledger, which he insists on.
