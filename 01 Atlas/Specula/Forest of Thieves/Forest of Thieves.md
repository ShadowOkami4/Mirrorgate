# Forest of Thieves

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Forests do not become infamous because of trees."*

> [!location] At a glance
> - **Kind** Dense old forest, about 90–130 km (55–80 miles) across
> - **Part of** The Southern Weald, of which it is the oldest and densest part
> - **Ruled by** Nobody who will admit it (see *Who owns the forest*)
> - **Roads** One: [[The Old Road]], running roughly north to south
> - **Known for** Bandits, travellers who arrive late or not at all, and lately glowing plants that do not belong to [[Kagameru]]
> - **Adventure** [[The Pact in the Twilight]] (SA-01)

## Overview

The **Forest of Thieves** is a dense, badly roaded wood on [[Specula]], far from the railway lines and from any capital. Branches crowd the one road through it, lantern light seems smaller under the canopy, and travellers trade rumours about drifting shards, mirror-touched ruins and strange flowers that glow between the roots like embers under ash.

Nobody lives inside the forest. Two or three small villages cling to its southern edge as the last outposts; [[Glasscombe]] is the best known of them, and [[The Last Lantern]] is the last tavern before the trees close in.

## Who owns the forest

On paper, the crown of [[Lustro]] claims the wood. In practice, nobody looks there. Strange lights and mirror-touched ruins are common in the forest, and every one of them contradicts the crown's official doctrine that [[The Mirrorgate]] does not exist. Rather than send patrols into a place that would force it to admit what it denies, the crown has chosen to forget the forest. The [[Lustral Office]] keeps no post there, the road is not maintained, and the thieves who gave the forest its name have flourished in the silence.

## Atmosphere

The forest should feel watchful before it feels monstrous. Use these details as the party travels:

- Bird calls stop a few seconds before the party reaches a place, then start again behind it.
- Luminous flowers grow thicker the further north the party goes; their light makes no shadows.
- Waystones along the road are carved with symbols that match the ancient railway.
- Large wolf tracks appear in the mud, then vanish where the shadows gather too thickly.

> [!gm]- Adventure role (SA-01)
> - Approach route to [[To the Eternal Lady]] and site of the opening bandit ambush
> - First visible sign of otherworldly contamination: the luminous plants
> - Shadow-wolf tracks that foreshadow later encounters
> - Route north to the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]

> [!rule] Useful checks
> - **Wisdom (Perception) DC 12:** notice movement or hidden watchers before the ambush.
> - **Intelligence (Nature) DC 13:** recognise that the luminous plants are not normal local growth.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 14:** sense planar resonance around the glowing roots.
> - **Wisdom (Survival) DC 14:** follow the wolf tracks until they vanish into shadow.

## Hooks

- A merchant pays well for an escort along the Old Road; half the goods he carries are smuggled.
- A Glasscombe family asks the party to find a son who went to pick "the flowers that glow".
- Someone has hung lanterns along a side path that was not there last week.
