# Glasscombe

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Glasscombe exists because the Old Road once needed a place to stop. The road has moved on. Glasscombe has not."*

> [!location] At a glance
> - **Kind** Small farming village, a few dozen people
> - **Where** On [[The Old Road]], at the southern edge of the [[Forest of Thieves]]
> - **Has** A handful of farms, a smithy, a chapel; [[The Last Lantern]] stands a short walk north

## Overview

**Glasscombe** is the last village on the Old Road before the forest. When the road was busy it was a prosperous stopping place; since the railway took the traffic away it has shrunk to a few farms, a smithy and a chapel. The villagers know the forest better than anyone, and fear it more.

The name is common tongue. Old villagers say a *combe*, a narrow valley, here once held water so still it looked like glass.

## Life in Glasscombe

- The villagers leave small offerings of bread and salt at the forest's edge, and will not say for whom.
- Thieves from the forest trade openly in the village; nobody dares object.
- The chapel bell is rung at dusk so that anyone still in the trees knows which way is home.

## Hooks

- A child from Glasscombe has started drawing the inside of a temple she has never seen.
- The smith has been asked to forge a key from a wax impression; the key fits no lock in the village.
