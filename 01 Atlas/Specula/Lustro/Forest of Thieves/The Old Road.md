# The Old Road

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Before the railway, every merchant in Lustro knew the Old Road. Now only thieves remember it."*

> [!location] At a glance
> - **Kind** Ancient trade road, roughly north to south
> - **Passes** [[Glasscombe]], [[The Last Lantern]], the [[Forest of Thieves]]
> - **State** Unmaintained and unpatrolled since the railway took its traffic

## Overview

**The Old Road** once carried much of the trade across the interior of [[Specula]]. When the railway came, the traffic moved to the rails and the road was left to decay. The stretch through the [[Forest of Thieves]] suffered most: without merchants, patrols or repairs, it became the hunting ground of the thieves who gave the forest its name. It is the only road through the forest.

## Along the road

- **Glasscombe and the Last Lantern.** The last village and the last tavern before the trees close in.
- **Waystones.** Old stones every few miles, carved with symbols that match the ancient railway. Nobody knows which came first.
- **The toll gate.** Thieves have rebuilt a ruined gatehouse and charge travellers for "protection".
- **The side path.** About 10–20 km into the forest, an unmarked path leaves the road and winds about 3 km to [[To the Eternal Lady]]. It is easy to miss and hard to find twice.

## Hooks

- A merchant convoy tries the Old Road to avoid the [[Lustral Office]]'s checks on the trains.
- A scholar wants rubbings of every waystone between Glasscombe and the temple.

> [!player] What players know
