# Lustro

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *“A crown may command a border. It cannot command what people remember seeing in the mirror.”*

**Lustro** is the central kingdom of [[Kagameru]], lying in the heartland continent of [[Specula]], and the most politically important realm currently documented in the setting. It is a kingdom of strict law, controlled public speech, royal ceremony, and carefully maintained official history. Its capital is [[Ogledal]].

The [[Blackwood Bloodline]] rules it: Queen [[Maria Blackwood]] holds the crown by birth, with [[Lucien Blackwood]] as her consort.

## The name

In the old tongue, *lustro* means *mirror*. In the older, more ceremonial language of the court, it means something else: *I survey, I review, I purify by rite*. The crown insists the second meaning is the correct one. Given what the kingdom does with its registers, census rolls, and approved histories, few people argue.

## Political identity

Lustro presents itself as orderly, lawful, stable, and protected by royal wisdom. Public life is shaped by hierarchy and obedience. The crown expects deference not only from nobles and soldiers, but from scholars, merchants, travelers, and common citizens.

Its laws are strict, and enforcement can be severe. Permits, oaths, travel papers, approved histories, and loyalty rituals may all be part of daily life depending on region and social class.

## The royal lie

The crown's most aggressive propaganda concerns [[The Mirrorgate]].

Officially, the Mirrorgate does not exist.

The court dismisses it as superstition, foreign corruption, criminal myth, tavern nonsense, or a dangerous metaphor spread by enemies of the kingdom. Scholars who study mirror-wounds too openly risk censure, disappearance, or quiet reassignment, usually at the hands of the [[Lustral Office]]. Witnesses are encouraged to recant. Families of victims are given official explanations that fit the kingdom's preferred version of reality.

This denial is not ignorance. The crown knows enough to be afraid.

> [!warning] Official doctrine
> In Lustro, publicly claiming that the Mirrorgate exists can be treated as sedition, heresy, madness, or foreign agitation depending on which official hears it first.

## The crown and the Blackwoods

The [[Blackwood Bloodline]] is Lustro's ruling dynasty. Queen [[Maria Blackwood]] reigns by birth, and her consort [[Lucien Blackwood]] is the visible hand of the crown's strict order. Both stand behind the campaign of denial around the Mirrorgate. Their children, [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]], do not approve, and both are watched carefully: their doubts make them dangerous to the crown and useful to anyone looking for cracks in the royal family.

## Opposition movements

Many groups work against the crown to reclaim their freedom. Some resist openly. Others survive through coded letters, hidden shrines, smuggled books, mirror-safehouses, false trade routes, and whispered testimony.

These groups are not unified. Some want reform. Some want revolution. Some only want missing relatives returned and forbidden truths acknowledged. Together they are known as [[The Unsilvered]]—a name first used by the crown as an insult and later adopted with pride.

See [[The Unsilvered]].

## Political themes

- strict royal law and social control
- propaganda denying the Mirrorgate
- noble families divided by loyalty and conscience
- underground organizations fighting for freedom
- forbidden scholarship around the [[Scars of the Primordial War]]
- disappearances connected to mirrors, sealed roads, and censored records
