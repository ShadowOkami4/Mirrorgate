# Skuggsjá

**Skuggsjá** (*SKOOG-syah*) is one of the six [[Kagameru#Continents|continents of Kagameru]]: the far north, a land of cold seas, hard winters and isolated holds.

## The name

An old northern word meaning *shadow-seer*: a mirror.
