# Scáthan

**Scáthan** (*SKAW-han*) is one of the six [[Kagameru#Continents|continents of Kagameru]]: a land of long nights, long shadowed coasts and planar distortion, lying at the edge of the known world as most maps draw it.

## The name

An old word for *mirror*, grown from the word for *shadow*.
