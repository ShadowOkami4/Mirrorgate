# The Pact in the Twilight

> [!info] Adventure
> - **Code** SA-01 · multishot
> - **Levels** starts at 4th, reaches 5th after Chapter 2
> - **Party** four characters (each encounter lists scaling for three and five)
> - **Rules** SRD 5.2.1; creatures use Atlas VTT statblocks
> - **Status** draft, about 30%: Prelude to Chapter 3 written, Chapter 4 not yet

## Summary

Strangers from distant roads each receive the same letter, asking them to come to an inn hidden in the [[Forest of Thieves]]. They meet at twilight, survive a band of frightened thieves, and reach [[To the Eternal Lady]], where the innkeeper [[Sandra Mistic]] reveals that she is a lich bound to guard a dangerous artifact.

Sandra cannot leave the inn. Her old friend [[Maria Rose]] sent a plea for help from a temple in the north, where strange glowing plants have begun to spread. Sandra asks the adventurers to go in her place.

The temple is not entirely in [[Kagameru]]. The [[Temple of the Moon in Water]] is one half of a place divided by [[The Mirrorgate]]; its other half is the [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]]. And somewhere in the twilight between the trees, something is hunting the party.

> [!gm] What the GM knows
> - Sandra's artifact reacted to each adventurer before she sent the letters. She does not know whether it chose them as protectors, liberators or victims.
> - The glowing plants grow from glasslike cracks in the earth. They are not native to Kagameru, or perhaps to any plane.
> - Maria Rose is a ghost. How she became one is not yet decided.
> - Sandra's wards are weakening faster than she admits. She suspects the disturbance is not trying to enter Kagameru, but to retrieve something already here.
> - Large wolf tracks circle the party. They vanish for several paces at a time, as though the creature passes through shadow itself.

## Running the adventure

- Chapters are played in order. Each holds its read-aloud text, checks and encounters; characters and places live in their own notes.
- Read-aloud text is in *read aloud* callouts, encounters in *combat* callouts, GM-only notes in *GM* callouts.
- Checks never block the story: the party always gets the map, the temple's location and Sandra's request.
