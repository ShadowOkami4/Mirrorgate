# Aurora

> *“A foreign god should not be made native merely because their story is beautiful.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Aurora** is a genderless deity of life worshiped as the central figure in the primary religion of the unknown world from which [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] came. Aurora is sometimes called a goddess, but the faith teaches that Aurora has no gender.

> [!important] Otherworld Religion
> Aurora is **not** the main deity of [[Kagameru]], and the faith of Aurora is **not** the main religion of the Mirrored Realms. This entry preserves what is known about the original world connected to Phineas Phantomhive I.

## The Faith of Aurora

The main religion of Phineas I's world teaches a way of life shaped by the will of Aurora, the deity of life. Aurora's followers do not necessarily deny the existence of other gods. Instead, they regard Aurora as the most important and most powerful deity.

According to surviving tradition, Aurora once descended upon a continent that was later named in their honor and brought life to the world.

## The World of Aurora

Aurora's world has no confirmed name in Kagameru's records. Almost everything known about it comes from the legends of Phineas Phantomhive I and from one traveler who crossed into it long ago through [[The Mirrorgate]] and returned.

- **Death is rarely undone.** Resurrection magic exists there, but it is difficult, costly, and uncommon. A faith devoted to a deity of life carries particular weight in a world where lives cannot easily be restored.
- **Syrid.** The traveler brought back word of a mysterious crystal-like substance called **Syrid**. Nothing more is known about it.

## Divine Intervention

Aurora is believed to intervene directly during dark times. Such intervention is rare, but the old accounts describe it as unmistakable.

Recorded examples include:

- granting one paladin several years of divine immortality
- sending one of Aurora's artifacts into the mortal world
- acting when the survival of life itself is threatened

These stories suggest that Aurora is not a distant or passive deity. Their power may appear when the world reaches a crisis severe enough to demand divine presence.

## Iconography

Aurora is often depicted as a serpent with:

- sharp teeth
- shining scales
- watchful eyes
- venom with healing properties

This image presents Aurora as both dangerous and life-giving. Their venom does not merely harm; in sacred art and religious teaching, it can heal.

## Known Uncertainties

- The name of Aurora's world remains unknown.
- The continent named after Aurora lies somewhere in that world; its location is unknown.
- It is unknown whether Aurora has any influence within Kagameru.
- Phineas I's home was destroyed by a dragon. Whether Aurora's faith played any part in what followed is unknown.
- What Syrid is, and whether it has anything to do with Aurora.

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - A relic of Aurora heals a dying creature but marks them with serpent-like eyes.
> - A paladin claims to carry Aurora's immortality for a limited number of years.
> - An artifact from Phineas I's world appears in Kagameru and behaves unlike local divine relics.
> - A cult mistakes Aurora for a Kagameru deity, creating a dangerous theological misunderstanding.
