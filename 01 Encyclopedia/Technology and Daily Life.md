# Technology and Daily Life

> *"Visitors from simpler worlds expect castles. Visitors from cleverer ones expect wonders. Kagameru disappoints both, then sells them a train ticket."*  
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

[[Kagameru]] stands between two ages. Most of the world still lives as it has for centuries: under crowns and noble houses, behind castle walls, in farming villages, guild towns, and temple cities. Yet in places, and above all on [[Specula]], it is a little more advanced than the worlds most adventurers know. Trains cross its heartland, and revolvers hang at the belts of a few travelers.

## Compared with Our World

In terms of our own history, Kagameru is a mix of the **Middle Ages** and the **early Industrial Revolution**.

| Like the Middle Ages | Like the early Industrial Revolution |
| --- | --- |
| kingdoms, crowns, and noble houses | railways across the heartland |
| castles, knights, and walled towns | steam locomotives on the newer lines |
| most people live on the land | early firearms, such as pepperboxes and revolvers |
| guilds, temples, and markets | |

> [!important] Not Steampunk
> Kagameru is **not** a steampunk world. There are no airships, no brass gadgets on every street, and no factories everywhere. Its advances are limited, uneven, and mostly concentrated in a few places. Away from the rails and the great cities, life looks much as it does in any traditional fantasy world.

## The Railways of Specula

The most advanced continent is [[Specula]], and its pride is its railway: a great and complicated network of lines that ties its realms together.

Not all of it is new. Some parts of the network are **ancient**, far older than any realm that uses them today.

- **The ancient lines** still run on a power that nobody fully understands. Their builders and their workings are not known.
- **The newer lines** were built in living memory, or close to it, and run on ordinary steam.

How much of the network is ancient, and who built the first lines, remains open.

## Firearms

Firearms exist in Kagameru, but their development took a different direction from our world's, and they are still **early in development**.

- **Pepperbox pistols** and **revolvers** are the best-known firearms.
- Some are faster than others; some are **magical**.
- They are **rare but not extremely rare**. Most soldiers, guards, and adventurers still rely on blades and bows, but a firearm will not shock anyone in a city.

Rules for firearms are not yet written.

## Open Questions

- Who built the ancient railway lines, and what powers them?
- How advanced are the other continents compared with Specula?
- Which other inventions exist: printing presses, mechanical clocks, telegraphs, or others?
- How did firearm development diverge from our world's?
