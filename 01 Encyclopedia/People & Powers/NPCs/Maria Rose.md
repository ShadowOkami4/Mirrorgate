## Overview

**Maria Rose** is a ghost and one of the few old friends still trusted by [[Sandra Mistic]]. She is the friend whose plea for help led Sandra to contact the adventurers.

Maria had been investigating the luminous plants and the planar disturbance surrounding the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]. That ruin is divided across realities, continuing within [[The Mirrorgate]] as [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]].

Maria's precise condition remains intentionally undefined. It is not yet established whether she was already a ghost when she began the investigation, died during it, or exists in a ghostlike state because the Mirrorgate has displaced her from her proper time and place.

## Relationship with Sandra

Maria is one of the rare people Sandra trusts enough to risk revealing her existence and the location of her inn. Their friendship predates the current disturbance, although its origin and age have not yet been established.

Sandra cannot leave [[To the Eternal Lady]] to answer Maria's plea herself. Her concern for Maria is therefore both personal and urgent, but the details of their shared past remain open for later development.

## What Is Established

- Maria Rose is a ghost.
- She is an old and trusted friend of Sandra Mistic.
- She investigated the unnatural plants near the northern ruin.
- A plea for help from Maria reached Sandra.
- She is connected to the Temple of the Moon in Water and the Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror.
