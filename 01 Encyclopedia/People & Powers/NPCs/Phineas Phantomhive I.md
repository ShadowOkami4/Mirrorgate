# Phineas Phantomhive I

> *"A name may cross worlds more easily than a man. Unfortunately, in this case, both appear to have done so."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Phineas Phantomhive I**, often called the **Original Phineas**, is one of the strangest figures connected to the [[Phantomhive Bloodline]]. He did not come from [[Kagameru]]. He was born in another world—a world whose people worship [[Aurora]], where the dead rarely return, and where a hero with no magic at all was remembered as one of the greatest of his age.

## Known Facts

- Phineas Phantomhive I came from a different world, the world of the faith of [[Aurora]].
- He was physically unable to use magic of any kind.
- His home was destroyed by a dragon.
- After that loss, he carried his family with him as other personalities, most notably his uncle **Jürgen** and his aunt **Elisabeth Phantomhive**, as he remembered them.
- He is remembered as a hero, traveling with [[Dwight]] and [[Thalrex Silberblick]].
- When Phineas I was at the same apparent age that [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]] is now, the two looked remarkably similar.
- The circumstances that brought his name, or the man himself, into Kagameru are unknown.

## A Hero Without Magic

Every legend of Phineas Phantomhive I agrees on one detail: he could not use magic. It was not a matter of talent or training. Something in him simply could not hold it. Spells he tried to cast came to nothing, and magical items that answered to anyone else lay dead in his hands.

The Professor, who rarely endorses a legend without three footnotes and a disclaimer, insists that this detail is true.

In a world of sorcerers and miracles, Phineas fought with what he had: nerve, cunning, stubbornness, and the two companions who never left his side. The contrast is part of every telling. [[Thalrex Silberblick]] could barely control the magic that poured out of him, and Phineas could not summon a spark—yet the stories insist that it was usually Phineas who knew what to do.

## The Dragon and the Family He Kept

Phineas's home was destroyed by a dragon. The legends do not agree on where the attack happened or why the dragon came, but they agree on what it left behind: ruins, and a young man who had lost everything he loved.

In Phineas's world, that loss was final. Resurrection magic existed there, but it was difficult, costly, and rare—far beyond the reach of an ordinary family.

Phineas found another way to keep his family. After the attack, other voices began to speak through him: members of his family, exactly as he remembered them. The best known are his uncle, **Jürgen Phantomhive**, and his aunt, **Elisabeth Phantomhive**. At times one of them would take his place entirely, speaking with their voice, moving with their habits, and offering the advice, comfort, or scolding they would have given in life.

The legends call this a split personality. They do not treat it as madness or a curse, but as grief that refused to let go. Phineas's companions learned to recognize who was speaking and to answer them by name.

> [!important] Not the Later Jürgen and Elisabeth
> The Jürgen and Elisabeth who lived in Phineas were his memories of his uncle and aunt in his own world. They are not the Jürgen and Elisabeth Phantomhive of later family records—although those two are, in turn, the uncle and aunt of [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]. Whether the names returned by family tradition or by something stranger is part of the [[Phantomhive Bloodline#The Recurrence Problem|Phantomhive recurrence]].

## The World He Left Behind

Phineas's world has no confirmed name in current records. What is known about it comes from Phineas's legends and from a single traveler who crossed into that world long ago through [[The Mirrorgate]] and, against all reasonable expectation, came back.

- **Faith.** Its main religion is the worship of [[Aurora]], a genderless deity of life.
- **Death.** Resurrection was possible but difficult, costly, and uncommon. For most people, death was final.
- **Syrid.** The traveler brought back word of a mysterious crystal-like substance called **Syrid**. Nothing more is known about it.

> [!important]
> The faith of Aurora is **not** the main religion of [[Kagameru]]. It is the main religion of the world of Phineas Phantomhive I.

## Resemblance to Phineas II

The resemblance between Phineas Phantomhive I and [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]] is difficult to dismiss. Records describe the first Phineas, at the relevant age, as nearly identical in appearance to the second.

This similarity may indicate ordinary inheritance, Mirrorgate distortion, a repeated soul-pattern, deliberate imitation, or something stranger. No conclusion is currently safe.

> [!question] Unresolved Mystery
> Was Phineas II named after the first because of family memory, or does the name itself return when certain conditions are met?

## Heroic Legends

The legends of Phineas Phantomhive I consistently place him in the company of two unusual companions.

The first is remembered only as [[Dwight]], a war medic and rogue: a battlefield healer who moved like a thief, or perhaps a thief who learned to keep people alive because the war gave him no better choice.

The second is named more fully: [[Thalrex Silberblick]], a dragonborn barbarian with strangely glowing blue eyes, who cast magic wildly, unpredictably, and without the discipline expected of trained mages.

The stories disagree on what the three fought, where they traveled, and whether their deeds ended in triumph, exile, or disaster. Some tellings connect their journey to the dragon that destroyed Phineas's home; others do not.

> [!note] Reliability
> These are legends, not confirmed biography. They should be treated as true enough to matter, but incomplete enough to become dangerous.

## Open Questions

- How did Phineas—or his name—come to Kagameru?
- What happened to Phineas's parents, and why are his uncle and aunt the family he remembered most strongly?
- Did Phineas ever find the dragon that destroyed his home?
- Why could he not use magic?
- What is Syrid, and did Phineas ever encounter it?
