# The Unsilvered

> *“Rebellion is rarely one fire. More often it is a hundred candles hidden from the same wind.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**The Unsilvered** are the anti-crown movements of [[Lustro]]. They are not a single army, faction, or ideology, but a loose collection of organizations, cells, families, scholars, defectors, smugglers, and local communities working against the strict rule of the crown.

Most of them want some form of freedom restored. What “freedom” means differs sharply between groups.

## The Name

A mirror without its silver backing is only glass: it reflects nothing, and you can see straight through it. The crown first used the word as an insult for people who refused to reflect the kingdom's approved image. The movements adopted it gladly. They consider transparency the whole point.

## Why They Resist

Common causes include:

- opposition to strict royal control
- anger over censorship and propaganda
- resentment of noble privilege
- refusal to accept the crown's denial of [[The Mirrorgate]]
- attempts to rescue people who disappeared after mirror-related incidents
- protection of forbidden histories tied to the [[Scars of the Primordial War]]
- desire for local autonomy from royal law

## The Mirrorgate Question

The crown insists the Mirrorgate does not exist. Anti-crown organizations often preserve proof that it does.

This proof may include:

- witness accounts
- forbidden maps
- mirror-scar locations
- names of disappeared people
- smuggled royal documents
- objects that age incorrectly
- testimonies from people who returned from impossible places

Possessing such material is dangerous. Sharing it is worse. The [[Lustral Office]] exists largely to make sure of both.

## Relationship to the Blackwoods

The movements do not agree on the [[Blackwood Bloodline]].

Some see all Blackwoods as symbols of oppression. Others believe [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]] may become allies because they dislike what their parents are doing. More radical groups consider this hope naive, arguing that royal blood remains royal blood no matter how guilty it feels.

## Adventure Use

- The party is asked to deliver forbidden proof of the Mirrorgate.
- A resistance cell tries to contact Jacob or Vanessa.
- A false resistance group is actually a crown trap.
- The party must choose between exposing the truth and protecting civilians from retaliation.
- A rebel archive contains records the Blackwoods thought destroyed.
