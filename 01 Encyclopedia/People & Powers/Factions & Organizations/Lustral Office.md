# Lustral Office

> *"No kingdom censors its history. It merely purifies it."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]], quoting an official of Lustro

The **Lustral Office** is the institution through which the crown of [[Lustro]] keeps its registers, approves its histories, and enforces its official doctrine that [[The Mirrorgate]] does not exist. Its armed agents are known as the **Lustral Guard**.

Its name comes from *lustration*: an old rite of purification. In Lustro, the rite is performed on records, testimony, and occasionally people.

## Duties

- keeping census rolls, travel papers, permits, and loyalty oaths
- approving public histories and school texts
- investigating "seditious superstition," especially claims about the Mirrorgate
- persuading witnesses to recant and supplying families with official explanations
- hunting [[The Unsilvered]]

## Open Questions

- Who commands the Office, and how close are they to [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]]?
- What does the Office actually know about the Mirrorgate?
- Where does it keep the evidence it confiscates?
