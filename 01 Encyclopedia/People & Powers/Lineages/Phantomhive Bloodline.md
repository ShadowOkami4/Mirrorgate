# Phantomhive Bloodline

> *“I assure you, my family is only mostly suspicious.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The **Phantomhive** name appears in an unusual number of records from Kagameru and beyond. It is not merely a noble surname; in some accounts, it behaves like a historical echo, recurring across regions, institutions, and even incompatible realities connected to [[The Mirrorgate]].

## Known Members

- [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] — the original Phineas: a hero from the world of [[Aurora]] who could not use magic.
- [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]
- **Jürgen Phantomhive** — uncle of Professor Phineas Phantomhive II; listed in older family fragments, current status requires confirmation.
- **Elisabeth Phantomhive** — aunt of Professor Phineas Phantomhive II; details not yet recorded.

The names Jürgen and Elisabeth appear twice in the family's story. Phineas I carried his uncle Jürgen and his aunt Elisabeth with him as other personalities—his memories of the family he lost when a dragon destroyed his home. The Jürgen and Elisabeth of later records are different people, and yet they hold exactly the same place in the family: they are the uncle and aunt of Phineas II.

## Reputation

The Phantomhives are most often associated with scholarship, strange inheritances, old houses, magical education, and inconvenient proximity to impossible events.

Depending on the source, the family has been described as:

- eccentric academics
- minor nobility with major secrets
- keepers of forbidden mirrors
- patrons of magical science
- recurring anomalies in Mirrorgate research

## The Recurrence Problem

The Phantomhive name appears in several accounts that should not share a common history. Some scholars argue this proves the family has been altered by the Mirrorgate. Others believe the name is a title, a mantle, or a marker placed upon certain individuals by forces not yet understood.

Professor Phineas, when asked directly, has described these theories as “deeply interesting, insufficiently proven, and personally inconvenient.”

One of the strongest pieces of evidence is the resemblance between [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] and [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]. When Phineas I was at the same apparent age that Phineas II is now, the two reportedly looked remarkably alike. This may be family resemblance, planar recurrence, or a sign that the Phantomhive name is tied to a repeating pattern.

The original Phineas came from an unknown world whose main religion centered on [[Aurora]], a genderless deity of life. This faith is not native to Kagameru.

The names of Phineas I's lost family have also returned. A Jürgen and an Elisabeth appear in later Phantomhive records, generations after the family Phineas remembered—and once again as the uncle and aunt of a Phineas. This may be nothing more than a family naming its children after its own legends. Scholars of the recurrence are not convinced.

## Adventure Use

- A sealed Phantomhive archive opens only for a living family member.
- A mirror shows a Phantomhive ancestor from a reality that never existed.
- Jürgen Phantomhive is found listed among the dead, the living, and the not-yet-born.
- A later Elisabeth Phantomhive begins speaking in the voice of the Elisabeth whom Phineas I remembered.
- A rival scholar claims the Phantomhive bloodline is not a family but a containment system.
