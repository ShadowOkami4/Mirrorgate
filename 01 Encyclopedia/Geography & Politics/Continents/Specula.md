# Specula

> *"A heartland is simply the place that has had the most time to argue about being one."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Specula** (*SPEK-yoo-lah*) is one of the six [[Continents of Kagameru]]. Surviving political fragments treat it as the heartland of Kagameru: a land of knightly houses, old kingdoms, and the oldest continuous royal claims.

## The Name

From an old word meaning both *mirror* and *watchtower*. Loyalists of [[Lustro]] prefer the second meaning. Everyone else suspects the first is more accurate.

## Known Places

- [[Lustro]] — the central kingdom of Kagameru and seat of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]]
