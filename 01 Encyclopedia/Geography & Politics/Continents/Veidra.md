# Veidra

> *"The fey are the only people I know who treat a mirror as an invitation rather than a convenience."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Veidra** (*VAY-drah*) is one of the six [[Continents of Zerkalo]]. It is usually linked to green realms, ancient groves, and cultures with strong ties to nature and fey-touched magic.

## The Name

From an old word meaning *face-shower*—the thing that shows you your face. Local tales add, unhelpfully, that it does not always show you *your* face.

## Known Places

No settlements have yet been recorded.
