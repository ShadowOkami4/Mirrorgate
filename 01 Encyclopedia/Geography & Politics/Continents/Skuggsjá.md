# Skuggsjá

> *"The north does not forgive a poor map. It simply buries the cartographer and waits for the next one."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Skuggsjá** (*SKOOG-syah*) is one of the six [[Continents of Zerkalo]]. It is associated with the far north: cold seas, hard winters, and isolated holds.

## The Name

Literally *shadow-seer*, an old northern word for a mirror. The name is also carried by a famous book of royal counsel in the north, which has done nothing to make it less ominous.

## Known Places

No settlements have yet been recorded.
