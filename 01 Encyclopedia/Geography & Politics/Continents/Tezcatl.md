# Tezcatl

> *"The south polished its first mirrors from volcanic glass. I am told they showed more than faces. I am told this with alarming sincerity."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Tezcatl** (*TESS-kaht*) is one of the six [[Continents of Zerkalo]]. It is associated with the sun-scorched south, stone cities, great temples, and rites older than most northern kingdoms.

## The Name

An old southern word for a mirror of polished obsidian. Such mirrors were used for scrying long before glass mirrors reached the south.

## Known Places

No settlements have yet been recorded.
