# Hansha

> *“A homeland is the place one spends youth trying to leave and age trying to explain.”*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

**Hansha** (*HAHN-shah*) is the greatest city of [[Specula]]: a **free city** of scholars, printers, engineers, and bankers, built across a ring of old hills. It belongs to no kingdom. It governs itself under an ancient charter and controls only the city and the farmland of the hills around it.

It is also the city most closely associated with [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]], who began the work that became the **Encyclopedia Kagami** in its [[Utsushi|academy quarter]].

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> Hansha was previously described as a hilly homeland region. It is now proposed as a free city, which fits its libraries, academies, and the Professor's story more logically. Details may still change.

## The Name

*Hansha* means *reflection* in an old tongue. The city likes to say it reflects the best of every land that trades with it. The Professor maintains that the name is a coincidence. No one else does.

## A Free City

Hansha is surrounded by the power of [[Lustro]], the strictest kingdom on Specula, and yet it is free. Three things keep it that way:

- **The railway.** Hansha's engineers built most of Specula's newer steam lines and are the only people who study the ancient lines seriously. The crown cannot run its trains without them.
- **Money.** Hansha's banking houses lend to half the courts of Specula, including Lustro's.
- **The charter.** Hansha's freedom rests on a charter older than the current kingdom of Lustro. Breaking it would raise awkward questions about how old Lustro's own claims really are.

## Hills and Halls

The city is built on and between its hills, each crowned by a different institution:

- **[[Utsushi]]**, the academy quarter, with its libraries, lecture halls, and copying-houses
- the **printing quarter**, whose presses print books that are banned across the border in Lustro
- the **engineers' guildhalls**, where the steam lines are planned and maintained
- the **counting-houses** of the great banks
- the **station**, where the line to [[Zrcalin]] begins, guarded on the Lustro side by [[The Watchful Gate]]

## Hansha and Lustro

The two powers need each other and distrust each other. Lustro censors; Hansha prints. Lustro denies [[The Mirrorgate]]; Hansha's scholars publish learned papers about it. Books printed in Hansha are among the most common forbidden goods smuggled into Lustro, and [[The Unsilvered]] find many friends in its printing quarter.

## Open Questions

- What does the ancient charter say, and who granted it?
- How is the city governed: by a council, the guilds, the banks, or the academies?
- Is Hansha the seat of the Phantomhive family's scholarly influence?
