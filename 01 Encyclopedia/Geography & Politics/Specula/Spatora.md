# Spatora

> *"The station is older than the city, the city is older than the kingdom, and nobody remembers who laid the first rail."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**Spatora** (*spah-TOR-ah*) is an old city built around one of the oldest surviving stations of Specula's ancient railway. Its great station hall, raised long before any living realm existed, still stands at the heart of the city, and the ancient line that runs through it has never stopped working.

## The Name

From an old word meaning *space* or *distance*: the city that once stood at the far end of the known line.

## Current Understanding

- a city that grew around an ancient station rather than the other way around
- famous for its station hall, built in a style no living architect can identify
- a centre of study for scholars who try to understand the ancient lines
- its realm and exact location are not yet decided

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - The station hall's great clock shows a time that has not yet happened.
> - A scholar has found a map of the ancient network that includes lines nobody has ever seen.
> - An ancient train arrives on a line that ends in solid rock.
