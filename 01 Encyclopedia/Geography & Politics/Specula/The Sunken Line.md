# The Sunken Line

> *"The tracks run straight into the hillside and down into the dark. Nobody has ever followed them to the end. Several people have tried."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**The Sunken Line** is an ancient railway line that leaves the rest of the network and descends into the earth through a great tunnel mouth. Unlike most ancient lines, no trains are seen on it. Its rails are polished, as if something still runs along them.

## Current Understanding

- an ancient line that descends underground into a tunnel of unknown length
- no trains are seen on it, yet its rails stay polished
- expeditions into it have turned back, or not returned
- its exact location is not yet decided

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - An expedition from Hansha asks the party to escort them to the first station below ground.
> - At the deepest point anyone has reached, the tunnel walls turn to mirror-bright stone.
> - A lamp sent down the line on an empty handcar comes back the next morning, still lit, with a stranger's note attached.
