# The Folded Road

> *"Travelers on the Folded Road arrive before they depart. The innkeepers at either end have learned to prepare rooms for guests who have not yet left."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

The **Folded Road** is an old road across a war-scar where distance has folded in on itself. A traveler who sets out along it may arrive hours before leaving, walk for a day and find themselves a mile from the start, or meet themselves coming the other way. An ancient railway line runs alongside it, and trains on that line are said never to keep the same timetable twice.

## Current Understanding

- a road across a scar where distance and time are folded
- travelers can arrive before they depart, or meet themselves on the way
- an ancient railway line runs beside it, with notoriously unreliable timetables
- its exact location is not yet decided

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - A messenger arrives with a letter the party has not yet written.
> - The party meets older versions of themselves on the road, who refuse to explain.
> - The rail line beside the road carries a train that left a century ago.
