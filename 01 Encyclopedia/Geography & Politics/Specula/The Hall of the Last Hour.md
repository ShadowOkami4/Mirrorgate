# The Hall of the Last Hour

> *"The monks keep a record of every hour, because in their valley, some hours come back."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

The **Hall of the Last Hour** is a monastery built at the edge of a war-scar where time repeats. Its monks keep careful records of every hour that passes, so they can tell which hours have happened before. They believe that watching broken time patiently is a form of devotion, and that someday the scar will repeat the hour in which it was made.

## Current Understanding

- a monastery on the edge of a time-looping scar (see [[The Repeating Vale]])
- its library holds centuries of records, some describing the same day hundreds of times
- the monks welcome scholars, but not those who want to "fix" the scar
- which faith or order the monks belong to is not yet defined

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - Two copies of the same monk arrive at the gate, each insisting the other is from a repeated hour.
> - The records predict an hour that has never happened before, and it is tomorrow.
> - Someone pays the party to steal a record that proves a crime happened in a repeated hour.
