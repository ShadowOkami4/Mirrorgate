# Forest of Thieves

> *“Forests do not become infamous because of trees.”*  
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

The **Forest of Thieves** is a dense, badly roaded woodland known for bandit activity, travelers who arrive late or not at all, and—more recently—the growing presence of luminous plants that do not belong to the natural order of [[Kagameru]].

It is the setting of [[The Pact in the Twilight]].

## Location

The forest lies deep in the interior of [[Lustro]], on [[Specula]], far from [[Ogledal]] and from the railway lines. It is the oldest and densest part of a much larger wood.

On paper, the forest belongs to the crown. In practice, nobody looks there. Strange lights, drifting shards, and mirror-touched ruins are common in the forest, and all of them contradict the crown's official doctrine that [[The Mirrorgate]] does not exist. Rather than send patrols into a place that would force it to admit what it denies, the crown has chosen to forget the forest. The [[Lustral Office]] keeps no post there, the roads are not maintained, and the thieves who gave the forest its name have flourished in the silence.

- **[[The Old Road]]** is the only road that crosses the forest. It was once a busy trade route; when the railway took its traffic away, it was left to decay, and the thieves moved in.
- **[[Glasscombe]]**, a small village on the Old Road, is the last settlement before the trees close in, and [[The Last Lantern]] is the last tavern.
- [[To the Eternal Lady]] currently stands in a sheltered hollow about 3 km off the Old Road, reached by an unmarked side path.
- The [[Temple of the Moon in Water]] lies in the forest's northern reaches, beside a spring.

## Atmosphere

The forest should feel watchful rather than openly monstrous at first. Branches crowd the road. Lantern-light seems smaller beneath the canopy. Strange flowers glow between roots like embers under ash.
