# The Back of the Glass

> *"A mirror without its backing is only glass. A tavern with the right backing is a revolution."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**The Back of the Glass** is a crooked roadside tavern on a back road near the border between [[Lustro]] and [[Hansha]]. To the [[Lustral Office]], it is a place of cheap ale and poor reputation. To [[The Unsilvered]], it is one of their oldest meeting places: books from Hansha's presses pass through its cellar on their way into the kingdom.

## The Name

A joke for those who understand it: *the back of the glass* is the silvering of a mirror, the very thing the Unsilvered have scraped away.

## Current Understanding

- a roadside tavern near the Lustro–Hansha border
- a secret meeting place and smuggling route of the Unsilvered
- watched, on and off, by the Lustral Office

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - The party is asked to carry a crate of books from the cellar past [[The Watchful Gate]].
> - A Lustral Office agent drinks at the tavern every night, and everyone pretends not to know.
> - The landlord needs to know which of his regulars is an informer.
