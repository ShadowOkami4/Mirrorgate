# The Watchful Gate

> *"At the Watchful Gate, every train from Hansha is searched for three things: smugglers, weapons, and ideas. The last are the hardest to find."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**The Watchful Gate** is a fortified checkpoint on the railway where the line from [[Hansha]] enters [[Lustro]]. Every train is stopped here, every passenger's papers are checked, and every crate is opened by officers of the [[Lustral Office]], who are looking above all for books printed in Hansha.

## Current Understanding

- a fortified rail checkpoint on the border between Lustro and the free city of Hansha
- run by the Lustral Office
- the main place where forbidden books are seized, and the main obstacle for those who smuggle them

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - The party's train is held at the gate while officers search for one particular passenger.
> - An officer at the gate sells passes to anyone who can pay.
> - A seized crate of books turns out to contain something the officers did not expect.
