# The Old Road

> *"Before the railway, every merchant in Lustro knew the Old Road. Now only thieves remember it."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**The Old Road** is a long, ancient road that once carried much of the trade across the interior of [[Lustro]]. When the railway came, the traffic moved to the rails, and the road was left to decay. The stretch through the [[Forest of Thieves]] suffered most: without merchants, patrols, or repairs, it became the hunting ground of the thieves who gave the forest its name.

## Current Understanding

- an ancient trade road, abandoned for the railway
- crosses the Forest of Thieves; [[Glasscombe]] is the last village before the forest
- unmaintained and unpatrolled; the crown has let it go
- [[To the Eternal Lady]] currently stands about 3 km off the road, in a hidden hollow

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - Old waystones along the road carry markings that match the ancient railway.
> - A merchant convoy tries the Old Road to avoid the Lustral Office's checks on the trains.
> - Thieves have rebuilt an old toll gate and charge travellers for "protection".
