# Odrava

> *"Odrava smells of salt, tar, and coal smoke, which is to say it smells of money."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**Odrava** (*OD-rah-vah*) is the chief seaport of [[Lustro]]. Ships from across the seas unload here, and the end of a steam line carries their cargo inland to [[Zrcalin]] and [[Ogledal]]. It is one of the few places in the kingdom where foreign sailors, merchants, and ideas arrive every day, which makes it one of the places the [[Lustral Office]] watches most closely.

## The Name

From an old word meaning *reflection*: the city where the harbour water mirrors the masts.

## Current Understanding

- Lustro's main seaport and the end of a steam line from the interior
- docks, warehouses, shipyards, and a busy foreign quarter
- customs houses where the Lustral Office searches cargo for forbidden books and mirrors
- a favourite landing place for smugglers

> - A ship arrives with a cargo of mirrors from another continent, and the Lustral Office wants every one destroyed.
> - Smugglers offer the party passage, at a price, on a ship the customs house never inspects.
> - The tide in Odrava's harbour starts reflecting a city that is not there.
