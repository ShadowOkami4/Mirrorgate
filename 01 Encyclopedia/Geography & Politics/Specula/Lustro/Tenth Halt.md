# Tenth Halt

> *"Tenth Halt is not a village that has a railway. It is a railway that accidentally grew a village."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**Tenth Halt** is a small village that grew up around a water stop on the steam line between [[Zrcalin]] and [[Cinderfold]]. Locomotives stop here to take on water from a great iron tank, and the village lives on the crews and passengers: a bakery, a few inns, a farrier, and farms that sell to the trains.

## The Name

A common-tongue name: it was simply the tenth stopping point on the line, and nobody ever bothered to give it a better one.

## Current Understanding

- a farming and railway village around a locomotive water stop
- most villagers work for the line or sell to its travellers
- the halt's stationmaster is the most important person in the village

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - A train stops at Tenth Halt and does not leave; its crew has vanished.
> - Passengers who step off at the halt for a minute return to find the train has been gone for a day.
> - The stationmaster keeps a second timetable, for trains that come along the ancient line nearby.
