# The Veiled Cathedral

> *"It is the only great church I know of where the mirrors are covered during prayer. The clergy insist this is about modesty. It is about the crown."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

The **Veiled Cathedral** is the great church of [[Ogledal]], capital of [[Lustro]]. Kings are crowned beneath its dome, and the crown's official faith is preached from its pulpit.

Its most famous custom gives it its name: during every service, every mirror and polished surface in the building is covered with a dark veil. Officially, this keeps the faithful from vanity. In truth, it began as one more part of the crown's denial of [[The Mirrorgate]].

## Current Understanding

- the coronation church of Lustro and seat of its official faith
- its mirrors are veiled during every service
- closely tied to the royal court and the [[Blackwood Bloodline]]
- which faith it serves is not yet defined
