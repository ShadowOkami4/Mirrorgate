# Zrcalin

> *"Every road in Lustro leads to Ogledal. Every rail leads to Zrcalin, which the capital finds deeply irritating."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**Zrcalin** (*ZR-chah-leen*) is the great railway city of [[Lustro]], and one of the busiest cities on [[Specula]]. It grew up where the ancient lines of the continent's railway meet the newer steam lines, and it lives on trains: their cargo, their passengers, their repairs, and their timetables.

## The Name

From an old word meaning *mirror*. Locals say the city earned it because the old lines and the new run side by side here, like a thing and its reflection.

## Current Understanding

- the chief railway junction of Lustro, where ancient and new lines meet
- a city of stations, freight yards, warehouses, and inns for travelers
- closely watched by the [[Lustral Office]], which checks the travel papers of everyone who boards
- home to rail workers who know more about the ancient lines than they are allowed to say
