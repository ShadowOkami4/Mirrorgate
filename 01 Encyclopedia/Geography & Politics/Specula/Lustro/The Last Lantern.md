# The Last Lantern

> *"Every traveller who enters the Forest of Thieves stops at the Last Lantern. The landlord counts them in. He tries not to count them out."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**The Last Lantern** is a lonely tavern on the Old Road, a short walk beyond [[Glasscombe]], where the road enters the [[Forest of Thieves]]. A lantern burns above its door day and night. Travellers say it is the last honest light before the forest.

## Current Understanding

- the last tavern before the Forest of Thieves
- run by a landlord who keeps a ledger of everyone who goes into the forest, and who comes back
- a place where travellers hire guides, and where guides refuse to go too far in
- the thieves of the forest leave it alone, by an old and unspoken agreement

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - The landlord's ledger shows a name that went into the forest forty years ago and came back last week.
> - A guide offers to take the party in, but only as far as the first glowing plants.
> - Someone has been leaving letters at the tavern for adventurers who have not arrived yet.
