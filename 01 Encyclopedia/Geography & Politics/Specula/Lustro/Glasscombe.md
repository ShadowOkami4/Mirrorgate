# Glasscombe

> *"Glasscombe exists because the Old Road once needed a place to stop. The road has moved on. Glasscombe has not."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

**Glasscombe** is a small farming village of [[Lustro]] on the **Old Road**, the last settlement before the trees close in around the [[Forest of Thieves]]. In the days when the Old Road was a busy route, Glasscombe was a prosperous stopping place. Since the railway took the traffic away, it has shrunk to a handful of farms, a smithy, and a chapel.

## The Name

A common-tongue name. Old villagers say a *combe*, a narrow valley, here once filled with water so still it looked like glass.

## Current Understanding

- the last village on the Old Road before the Forest of Thieves
- poor, quiet, and largely forgotten by the crown
- the villagers know the forest better than anyone, and fear it more
- [[The Last Lantern]] stands a short walk beyond the village, at the forest's edge

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - The villagers leave offerings at the forest's edge and refuse to say for whom.
> - A child from Glasscombe has started drawing the inside of a temple she has never seen.
> - Thieves from the forest trade openly in the village, and the villagers are too afraid to object.
