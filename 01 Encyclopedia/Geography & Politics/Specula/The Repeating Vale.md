# The Repeating Vale

> *"In the Vale, it is always the same autumn. The apples never fall, and the harvest festival never ends."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

The **Repeating Vale** is a farming valley caught in a time loop. The same autumn week repeats there again and again, and the villagers inside it do not notice. To them, every harvest festival is the first. Travelers who stay long enough begin to notice the same conversations, the same weather, and the same accidents.

The [[The Hall of the Last Hour|Hall of the Last Hour]] stands at the valley's edge, watching.

## Current Understanding

- a valley where one autumn week has repeated for generations
- the villagers inside do not realize the week repeats
- visitors are not caught in the loop, but they can be changed by it
- what started the loop, and whether it can be ended, are unknown

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - A villager has begun to remember the previous weeks, and is terrified.
> - Ending the loop would let a week of disasters finally finish happening.
> - Someone outside the Vale is using the loop to hide something that must never age.
