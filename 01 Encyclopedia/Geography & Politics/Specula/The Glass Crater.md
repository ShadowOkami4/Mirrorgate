# The Glass Crater

> *"Everyone who visits the crater says it is beautiful. Most of them say it again, a few seconds later, without remembering the first time."*
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> [!warning] Provisional Draft
> This entry is a draft proposal. Its name, location, and details may change, and it does not yet appear on the world map.

The **Glass Crater** is one of the best-known [[Scars of the Primordial War]] on [[Specula]]: a vast bowl of land turned to dark, glasslike stone, as if something struck the ground with the force of a falling star. Drifting shards turn in the air above it in great numbers, and its surface reflects the sky so perfectly that birds sometimes fly into it.

## Current Understanding

- a wide crater of glasslike stone; its creation is placed in the Primordial War
- drifting shards gather above it in unusual numbers
- visitors sometimes repeat words or actions a few seconds later without noticing
- its exact location is not yet decided

> [!gm]- Adventure Use
> - Treasure hunters chip glass from the crater to sell as mirrors. The mirrors start showing the crater.
> - A party camping at the rim wakes to find the camp of the night before still burning beside them.
> - Mirror-touched creatures crawl from a crack at the crater's heart.
