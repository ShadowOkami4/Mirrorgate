# Specula

> *"A heartland is simply the place that has had the most time to argue about being one."*  
> —Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

**Specula** (*SPEK-yoo-lah*) is one of the six [[Continents of Kagameru]]. Surviving political fragments treat it as the heartland of Kagameru: a land of knightly houses, old kingdoms, and the oldest continuous royal claims.

Specula is also the most advanced continent of Kagameru. A great and complicated railway network ties its realms together, and some of its lines are ancient, running on a power nobody fully understands. See [[Technology and Daily Life]].

## The Name

From an old word meaning both *mirror* and *watchtower*. Loyalists of [[Lustro]] prefer the second meaning. Everyone else suspects the first is more accurate.

## How Specula Works

> [!note] Provisional
> This overview describes the proposed structure of Specula. It is built for logic and playability, and details may change.

- **Power.** [[Lustro]] is the largest and strictest kingdom on the continent. The free city of [[Hansha]] is its rival in everything but arms. Other, smaller realms exist; their names are provisional.
- **The railway.** The rails tie the continent together. The ancient lines are older than any realm; the newer steam lines were built by Hansha's engineers and are fuelled by coal from [[Cinderfold]]. Because the trains carry most trade, the cities along the lines grew rich, and the places away from them, such as the [[Forest of Thieves]] and the [[The Old Road|Old Road]], were left behind.
- **Trade.** Goods come in by sea through [[Odrava]], move by rail through [[Zrcalin]], and are paid for through the banks of Hansha.
- **Control.** The [[Lustral Office]] watches the rails, the ports, and the border with Hansha at [[The Watchful Gate]]. It does not watch the places it would rather forget.
- **Life.** Most people still live in farming villages, such as [[Glasscombe]] and [[Tenth Halt]]. Industry is rare: Cinderfold's mines and ironworks are almost the only example.
