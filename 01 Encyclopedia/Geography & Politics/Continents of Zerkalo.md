# Continents of Zerkalo

> *"One must be careful with old maps. They are often correct about mountains and terribly wrong about everything important."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The surviving atlas fragments of [[Zerkalo]] name six great landmasses. Scholars have long noticed something curious about these names: in the oldest tongues of the world, every one of them means *mirror*. Whether the first cartographers were poets, pessimists, or simply well informed remains a matter of debate.

| Continent | Pronunciation | Character | Name meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Specula]] | SPEK-yoo-lah | heartland of knightly houses and old kingdoms | "mirror" and "watchtower" |
| [[Drychwen]] | DRUKH-wen | old forests, noble settlements, early magical traditions | "fair mirror" |
| [[Scáthan]] | SKAW-han | long nights, shadowed coasts, planar distortion | "mirror," from "shadow" |
| [[Veidra]] | VAY-drah | green realms, ancient groves, fey-touched magic | "face-shower" |
| [[Skuggsjá]] | SKOOG-syah | cold seas, hard winters, isolated holds | "shadow-seer" |
| [[Tezcatl]] | TESS-kaht | sun-scorched south, stone cities, temples, older rites | "mirror" of polished obsidian |

> [!warning] Provisional Canon
> The continents are working references. Their borders, relative positions, and peoples have not yet been mapped.

## Tensions Across the Map

Even incomplete records show recurring causes of conflict:

- competition for ruins caught in broken time
- noble claims based on half-remembered bloodlines
- religious disputes over visible scars of [[The Primordial War]]
- expeditions vanishing near mirror-linked sites
- border conflicts sparked by newly uncovered ruins
