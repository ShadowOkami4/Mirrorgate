# Utsushi

> *"Every scholar in Utsushi is either copying a book, correcting a copy, or arguing that the original was wrong."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Utsushi** (*oo-TSOO-shee*) is an academy-town of archives and lecture halls in [[Hansha]]. It is where [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]] began the work that became the **Encyclopedia Kagami**.

## The Name

The name means *copy*, *transcription*, and *reflection* all at once. Its scribes consider this an honest description of scholarship: every copy reflects the original, and every reflection changes it a little.

## Current Understanding

- a town built around academies, copying-houses, and old libraries
- the scholarly home of the Professor's early studies
- a natural starting point for expeditions and research
- a possible seat of the Phantomhive family's scholarly influence

## Open Questions

- Which academies are based here, and who funds them?
- Do any of Utsushi's archives hold records the scholars would rather not copy?
