# Ogledal

> *"In Ogledal, one is never alone. This is presented as a comfort."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Ogledal** (*OG-leh-dahl*) is the royal capital of [[Lustro]] and the seat of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]]. It is a city of ceremony, registries, and polished stone, where public squares are arranged so that very little happens out of sight.

## The Name

The name comes from an old word for *mirror*, built on a root meaning *to look over* or *to inspect*. Visitors tend to learn both meanings within their first day.

## Current Understanding

- the capital of Lustro and centre of royal ceremony
- home of the royal court and the principal Blackwood residences
- headquarters of the [[Lustral Office]]
- rumoured to contain a mirror-linked archive that official maps insist does not exist
