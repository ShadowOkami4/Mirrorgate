# Life in the Mirrorgate

> *"I expected ghosts. I found a meadow full of deer, all of them watching me in a way that suggested I was the ghost."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

[[The Mirrorgate]] is not a graveyard of reflections. Life has taken root among its shards: plants that drink reflected light, animals that know which way is down on every island, and creatures that swim through the open sky of the Drift. They were born behind the glass, and most have never seen any other world.

## Natives and Shardkin

Travelers often assume that everything living in the Mirrorgate is a monster. Scholars distinguish two very different kinds of inhabitant.

| | Natives | [[Shardkin]] |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Origin** | born and evolved in the Mirrorgate | made from broken reflections of people, moments, or images from elsewhere |
| **Nature** | living creatures with young, life cycles, and ecosystems | fragments: lonely, hungry, and unnatural |
| **Place in the plane** | at home | out of place, even here |

Most natives avoid shardkin. A few hunt them.

## Living Behind the Glass

Mirrorgate life has adapted to a world of borrowed light, shifting gravity, and loose time.

- **Light.** With no sun, plants feed on the light of the cracked sky. Many creatures have mirror-bright scales, bark, or feathers that gather and throw back that light.
- **Gravity.** Every shard has its own *down*. Native animals feel the change instinctively and cross from one shard to the next without falling—or simply fly, float, or swim through the Drift.
- **Time.** Many creatures sense places where time slips and avoid them. Others use them: some are said to raise their young on shards where time runs slow, so that a season of safety lasts for years.
- **Mirrors.** Some natives can see through mirrors into the material world. Very few can cross.

## Plants

- **Silverbark** — trees whose bark is polished like a mirror. A silverbark grove dazzles in the light and hides anything standing still within it.
- **Hangwood** — the upside-down forests reflected from lakes, which have taken root and grow downward from the undersides of shards.
- **Lanternweed** — glowing plants that drift through the Drift on long trailing roots, like jellyfish made of leaves.
- **Seam moss** — a faintly glowing moss that grows along the broken edges of shards.

## Animals

- **Driftwhales** — vast, slow creatures that swim through the Drift between shards. Older driftwhales carry small shards of land on their backs, complete with trees and grazing animals.
- **Silverhorns** — deer with antlers like polished glass. A silverhorn's reflection appears a moment before the animal does, which makes them extraordinarily hard to ambush.
- **Upfall salmon** — fish that leap up the Mirrorgate's rising waterfalls to spawn on the shard above.
- **Twinwings** — birds that always fly in mirrored pairs, one perfectly copying the other.
- **Seam beetles** — small beetles that live in the cracks of shards and glow brighter the more they eat. Travelers keep them in jars as lanterns.
- **Echo foxes** — clever foxes that mimic sounds heard through mirrors from the material world, sometimes in the voice of someone in a room on the other side.
