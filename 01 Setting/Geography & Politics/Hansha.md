# Hansha

> *“A homeland is the place one spends youth trying to leave and age trying to explain.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Hansha** is the homeland most closely associated with [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]] and the early work that eventually became the **Encyclopedia Kagami**.

Current records describe Hansha as a place of familiar hills, respectable halls, and enough libraries to give a young scholar the dangerous impression that the world can be understood from a desk.

## Current Role

Hansha presently functions as:

- the professor's scholarly point of origin
- a possible home for academies, archives, and old lecture halls
- a civilized contrast to the stranger roads of [[Zerkalo]]
- a starting point for expeditions into less understood regions

## Open Questions

The exact political status of Hansha remains undecided. It may become:

- a city-state
- a region within a larger kingdom
- an academy-town
- the seat of the Phantomhive family's scholarly influence
- a mirror-touched settlement whose history is less stable than it appears
