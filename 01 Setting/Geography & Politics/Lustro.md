# Lustro

> *“A crown may command a border. It cannot command what people remember seeing in the mirror.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Lustro** is the central kingdom of [[Zerkalo]] and the most politically important realm currently documented in the setting. It is a kingdom of strict law, controlled public speech, royal ceremony, and carefully maintained official history.

The [[Blackwood Bloodline]] is one of Lustro's most important royal families.

## Political Identity

Lustro presents itself as orderly, lawful, stable, and protected by royal wisdom. Public life is shaped by hierarchy and obedience. The crown expects deference not only from nobles and soldiers, but from scholars, merchants, travelers, and common citizens.

Its laws are strict, and enforcement can be severe. Permits, oaths, travel papers, approved histories, and loyalty rituals may all be part of daily life depending on region and social class.

## The Royal Lie

The crown's most aggressive propaganda concerns [[The Mirrorgate]].

Officially, the Mirrorgate does not exist.

The court dismisses it as superstition, foreign corruption, criminal myth, tavern nonsense, or a dangerous metaphor spread by enemies of the kingdom. Scholars who study mirror-wounds too openly risk censure, disappearance, or quiet reassignment. Witnesses are encouraged to recant. Families of victims are given official explanations that fit the kingdom's preferred version of reality.

This denial is not ignorance. The crown knows enough to be afraid.

> [!warning] Official Doctrine
> In Lustro, publicly claiming that the Mirrorgate exists can be treated as sedition, heresy, madness, or foreign agitation depending on which official hears it first.

## The Crown and the Blackwoods

The Blackwoods stand close to the center of royal power. Current working records identify [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]] as the parents of [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]].

Lucien and Maria appear aligned with the crown's strict rule and its campaign of denial surrounding the Mirrorgate. Jacob and Vanessa do not approve of what their parents are doing, but both are watched carefully. Their doubts make them dangerous to the crown—and useful to anyone seeking cracks inside the royal family.

## Opposition Movements

Many groups work against the crown to reclaim their freedom. Some resist openly. Others survive through coded letters, hidden shrines, smuggled books, mirror-safehouses, false trade routes, and whispered testimony.

These groups are not unified. Some want reform. Some want revolution. Some only want missing relatives returned and forbidden truths acknowledged.

See [[Anti-Crown Movements of Lustro]].

## Political Themes

- strict royal law and social control
- propaganda denying the Mirrorgate
- noble families divided by loyalty and conscience
- underground organizations fighting for freedom
- forbidden scholarship around the [[Scars of the Primordial War]]
- disappearances connected to mirrors, sealed roads, and censored records

## Campaign Use

- The party is punished for speaking too openly about the Mirrorgate.
- A royal decree sends adventurers toward a war-scar while denying why it matters.
- Jacob or Vanessa secretly contacts the party under surveillance.
- An anti-crown organization asks the party to smuggle proof that the Mirrorgate exists.
- Lucien or Maria uses royal authority to suppress a dangerous truth.
- The capital contains a mirror-linked archive that official maps insist does not exist.
