# Afterimage

> *"I once watched one strike a man who had already walked away. He felt it anyway, two seconds later. He was very cross about it."*
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

An **afterimage** is a moment that got left behind. It looks like a person-shaped smear of pale light, the kind of shape that lingers in the eye after staring too long at a lamp, and it lives two seconds behind the rest of the world.

Afterimages form where time has been damaged: in ruins where the hours repeat, beside clocks that run backward, and in rooms where something terrible happened too quickly to finish. They endlessly replay the last motions of whoever cast them, and they react violently to anyone who interrupts the replay. Their blows land twice—once when they swing, and again a moment later, when the rest of the world catches up.

### Traits

**Out of Step.** The afterimage doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks. Creatures react to where it was a moment ago, not where it is.

### Actions

**Echoing Blow.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Force damage. The blow echoes: at the start of the afterimage's next turn, the target takes 6 (1d6 + 3) Force damage again, unless it is no longer within 5 feet of the space it occupied when it was hit.

## Running Afterimages

Describe the echo clearly the first time it happens—"you feel the blow start to land a second time"—so players learn that stepping away avoids it. An afterimage that is left alone simply repeats its moment: a woman reaching for a door, a guard turning at a noise. Watching the full replay can reveal what happened here.
