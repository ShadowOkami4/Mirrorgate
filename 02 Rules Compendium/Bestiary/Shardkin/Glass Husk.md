# Glass Husk

> *"The body remembers how to walk long after it has forgotten why."*
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

A **glass husk** is the body left behind when a person is shattered by a mirror. The soul is gone; in its place, something jagged has moved in. Thin plates of glass push through the skin like frost across a windowpane, and the husk's joints grind with the sound of a mirror being scratched.

Husks are slow, stubborn, and single-minded. They often return to the place where they broke—a bedroom, a hall of mirrors, a ruined shrine—and stand before the glass as if waiting for it to give something back. When destroyed, a husk bursts apart in a spray of razor-edged fragments.

## Glass Husk
*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*

**AC** 12 (glass plating) · **Initiative** −1 (9)
**HP** 22 (3d8 + 9)
**Speed** 25 ft.

### Traits

**Burst of Glass.** When the husk dies, it bursts into razor-edged fragments. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the husk. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Slashing damage. *Success:* Half damage.

### Actions

**Glass Limb.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Slashing or Bludgeoning damage (husk's choice).

### Bonus Actions

**Faint Reflection (1/Day).** The husk creates a dim duplicate of itself in its space that lasts for 1 minute. While the duplicate lasts, whenever a creature makes an attack roll against the husk, roll 1d20. On an 11 or higher, the attack hits the duplicate instead, destroying it.

## Running Glass Husks

Husks walk straight toward the nearest living creature and keep swinging. Their bursting death rewards players who fight them at reach or with spells, and punishes those who crowd around them.
