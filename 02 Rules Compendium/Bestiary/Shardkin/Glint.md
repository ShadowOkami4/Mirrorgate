# Glint

> *"They are drawn to warmth, to voices, and—most unsettlingly—to people who are dying. I prefer to believe they are only lonely."*
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

A **glint** is a splinter of a shattered soul: a flickering mote of light about the size of a child, barely aware of itself and painfully lonely. It drifts toward warmth and voices, circling the living as if trying to remember what being one of them was like.

Glints are rarely alone. A shattered soul breaks into several of them, and they tend to stay near the [[Glass Husk]] and [[Remnant]] that were once the rest of the same person. When a glint is destroyed, those nearby sometimes hear a single whispered word—usually a name.

### Traits

**Weightless Light.** The glint can move through the spaces of other creatures. It can't willingly end its move in a space occupied by more than one other glint.

**Drawn to the Dying.** At the start of each of its turns, if the glint is within 10 feet of a creature that has 0 Hit Points, it gains a cumulative +1 bonus to its attack rolls (maximum +3). The bonus lasts for 1 minute.

### Actions

**Cold Touch.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Psychic damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

### Reactions

**Flicker (1/Day).** *Trigger:* The glint is hit by an attack roll. *Response:* The glint vanishes in a flash and teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 15 feet. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature within 5 feet of the space it left. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.

## Running Glints

Glints swarm the nearest warm body and dart away when struck. They prefer targets that are already hurt, and they gather around anyone who falls unconscious. A character who speaks kindly to a glint—or says its name, if the party has learned it—may make it hesitate for a round, at the GM's discretion.
