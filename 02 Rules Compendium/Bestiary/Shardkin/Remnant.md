# Remnant

> *"It remembers being someone. It is the remembering that makes it cruel."*
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

A **remnant** is the self that survives when a person is shattered by a mirror: the part that remembers a name, a face, and a life, cut loose from the body and soul that once carried it. It looks like its former self seen through cracked glass—the right shape, but split along a dozen jagged seams that shift when it moves.

Remnants remember being whole, and they cannot forgive anyone who still is. They speak in stolen fragments of their old voice, wear the gestures of their old life, and try to break others the way they were broken—by showing them a reflection they cannot bear to fight.

### Traits

**Splinter (1/Turn).** Immediately after the remnant hits a creature with Rend Memory, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 2 (1d4) Necrotic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes against a spell before the end of its next turn.

**Last Shard.** When the remnant dies, it leaves behind a glowing shard of itself in its space: a Tiny object with AC 10 and 1 Hit Point. At the start of what would have been the remnant's next turn, the shard explodes and disappears. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the shard. *Failure:* The target is affected as if by the *Confusion* spell until the end of its next turn. If the shard is destroyed before it explodes, it vanishes harmlessly.

### Actions

**Rend Memory.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Psychic damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target suffers a brief memory fracture until the end of its next turn. The GM chooses the effect, such as forgetting a prepared response, failing to recognize an ally, losing access to one language, or being unable to recall why it entered the room.

### Bonus Actions

**Mirror of Self (1/Day).** The remnant conjures an unstable double of one creature it can see within 30 feet. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, the target. *Failure:* Until the end of its next turn, the target believes it is fighting itself. It has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than the remnant and can't willingly move farther away from the remnant.

## Running Remnants

A remnant opens with Mirror of Self against the strongest-looking warrior, then fights beside its husk and glints. It taunts in scraps of its old voice—half a lullaby, a shopkeeper's greeting, a name it can't finish. Clever players can use these fragments to learn who it used to be. Warn the party about the Last Shard by letting the remnant's seams glow brighter as it weakens.
