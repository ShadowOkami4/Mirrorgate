# Scar Hazards

> *"Some battlefields are buried. Others continue happening."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

This module gives Game Masters tools for running the [[Scars of the Primordial War]] at the table: places where time, space, memory, or reflection were wounded and never healed properly. The lore of the scars lives in the encyclopedia entry; this page covers only how they play.

> [!warning] Playtest Draft
> These rules are a first draft. DCs and effects will be adjusted through playtesting.

## Identifying a Scar

Use these checks when characters investigate a possible war-scar.

| Check | DC | What a success reveals |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Wisdom (Perception) | 12 | A repeated sound, shadow, reflection, or motion |
| Intelligence (Investigation) | 13 | Physical evidence that two different times or locations overlap |
| Intelligence (Arcana) | 14 | Temporal or spatial distortion rather than ordinary spell residue |
| Intelligence (History) | 15 | A link between the site and a mythic account of [[The Primordial War]] |
| Wisdom (Insight) | 16 | A memory, instinct, or emotion here does not belong to the character feeling it |
| Wisdom (Survival) | 17 | Paths, distances, or landmarks here cannot be mapped consistently |

A scar should rarely be solved by a single check. Let successful checks reveal *how* the place is wrong, not immediately how to fix it.

## Scar Effects

Choose an effect that suits the site, or roll 1d8. A scar usually has one dominant effect; larger or older scars may have two.

| 1d8 | Effect |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Time Loop |
| 2 | Spatial Fold |
| 3 | Delayed Ruin |
| 4 | Memory Bleed |
| 5 | Age Storm |
| 6 | Mirror Wound |
| 7 | Chronal Ghosts |
| 8 | Roll twice, rerolling further 8s |

**Time Loop.** A few seconds keep repeating. At the start of each round, roll 1d6. On a 1, every creature inside the scar returns to the space it occupied at the start of the previous round. Hit points, spent resources, and conditions are unchanged; only position rewinds.

**Spatial Fold.** Two points within the scar, or one inside and one far away, are joined. A creature that enters a folded space leaves from its partner, as though teleported. A successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals where a fold begins and ends.

**Delayed Ruin.** A structure is collapsing one stone at a time across centuries. On initiative count 20 of each round, part of the ceiling or wall falls onto one 5-foot square chosen by the GM. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, any creature in that square. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage. *Success:* Half damage. The square becomes Difficult Terrain.

**Memory Bleed.** A creature that finishes a Short or Long Rest inside the scar makes a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. *Failure:* It wakes holding a memory that is not its own—a genuine clue, a false lead, or a glimpse of a life that was never lived—and has Disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks until it finishes a Long Rest outside the scar.

**Age Storm.** Time moves unevenly through flesh and material. Once per hour spent inside the scar, each creature makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. *Failure:* The creature gains 1 Exhaustion level and visibly ages or grows younger by 1d10 years. The change in apparent age fades after a Long Rest outside the scar.

**Mirror Wound.** Reflective surfaces open toward [[The Mirrorgate]]. A creature that touches a reflective surface in the scar makes a DC 14 Charisma saving throw. *Failure:* The creature is pulled through to a location of the GM's choice within the Mirrorgate. Once per hour, a reflection may instead step out of a surface—use the [[Glint]] stat block, or the [[Counterfeit]] for a more dangerous reflection.

**Chronal Ghosts.** Echoes of people caught in a moment that refuses to end replay their final actions. They are harmless unless disturbed. A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check reveals which moment they are trapped in. A creature that interrupts an echo's final moment may turn it hostile—use the [[Afterimage]] stat block.

## Leaving a Scar

Most scar effects end as soon as a creature leaves the affected area. Effects that last longer say so. Whether a scar can ever be closed, sealed, or healed is a question for individual adventures.
