# Scarborn Sorcery

> *"Most sorcerers inherit their magic from a dragon, a storm, or an ancestor with poor judgment. The Scarborn inherit theirs from a wound in the world. I am told it itches."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

*Sorcerer Subclass · Carry a Wound in Time and Space*

Scarborn sorcerers carry a wound in reality inside themselves: a small crack where time runs unevenly, distance folds, and reflections stop obeying the people who cast them. Some were born near a place where the world was broken; others inherited the crack from an ancestor who touched something they should not have. Where a Scarborn sorcerer walks, seconds stutter, mirrors lag, and the space between two points is never quite as wide as it should be.

> [!warning] Playtest Draft
> This subclass is a first draft. Its features may change through playtesting.

## Signs of the Scar

Roll on the table or choose how your scar shows itself.

| 1d6 | Sign |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Your reflection moves a fraction of a second after you do—or, on bad days, before. |
| 2 | Clocks and hourglasses near you run fast, slow, or backward when you are upset. |
| 3 | Your footprints sometimes appear a few steps ahead of you. |
| 4 | You occasionally answer questions a moment before they are asked. |
| 5 | A thin, glasslike line runs across your skin and glows faintly when you cast spells. |
| 6 | In your reflection, you are always standing somewhere slightly different. |

## Level 3: Scarborn Spells

The broken time and folded space inside you grant access to certain spells. When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Scarborn Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
| ---: | --- |
| 3 | *Feather Fall*, *Mirror Image*, *Misty Step*, *Silent Image* |
| 5 | *Blink*, *Slow* |
| 7 | *Banishment*, *Dimension Door* |
| 9 | *Scrying*, *Teleportation Circle* |

## Level 3: Mirror Sense

Reflections, hours, and distances are never quite strangers to you. You gain the following benefits.

**Glass Focus.** You can use a handheld mirror as a Spellcasting Focus for your Sorcerer spells.

**Unerring Moment.** You always know the time of day and which direction is north.

**Trace the Fold.** When a creature you can see teleports, you know the space where it arrives, provided that space is within 60 feet of you.

## Level 3: Moment Slip

Your spells tear a small gap in space, and you step through it. Once on each of your turns, immediately after you cast a spell using a spell slot, you can teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.

## Level 6: Stolen Second

You can snatch a moment back from the world and let it happen again. When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes a D20 Test, you can take a Reaction and spend 2 Sorcery Points after the roll but before its outcome is determined. The creature rerolls the d20 and must use the new roll.

## Level 6: Fold Space

You can fold distance like a crease in a map. As a Bonus Action, you can spend 2 Sorcery Points to link two unoccupied 5-foot squares you can see within 60 feet of you. The fold lasts for 1 minute, until you have the Incapacitated condition, or until you use this feature again.

When you or a creature of your choice enters one of the linked squares, that creature can immediately teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the other square. A creature can use the fold only once per turn.

## Level 14: Living Scar

When you activate Innate Sorcery, broken time spills out of you. For the duration, you gain the following benefits.

**Dragging Hour.** Each creature of your choice that starts its turn within 15 feet of you has its Speed halved until the start of its next turn.

**Slipstream.** You can use Moment Slip immediately after casting any spell, including a cantrip, and it can teleport you up to 30 feet.

## Level 18: Shatter the Hour

You can break the moment around you like glass. As a Magic action, you release a burst of broken time. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* your spell save DC, each creature of your choice in a 30-foot Emanation originating from you. *Failure:* 4d10 Force damage, and the target is frozen in a moment that refuses to end: it has the Incapacitated condition and a Speed of 0 until the start of your next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest unless you spend 5 Sorcery Points (no action required) to restore your use of it.

## In the Mirrored Realms

> [!info] Optional Setting Lore
> The rules of this subclass work in any fifth-edition world. This section describes how it fits the setting of the Mirrored Realms and can be ignored elsewhere.

Scholars disagree on how a person becomes Scarborn. The most common account is simple: they were born within a scar, or close enough for the scar to notice them. Other accounts speak of children who wandered into a time loop and came out a heartbeat out of step with the world, or families whose reflections have misbehaved for generations.

Some theologians go further, claiming that Scarborn magic is the residue of the Prime Gods who died at the end of [[The Primordial War]]. The Scarborn tend to find this theory either flattering or terrifying, depending on the day.

In [[Lustro]], a Scarborn sorcerer is living proof that the kingdom's official history is incomplete. The [[Lustral Office]] keeps a quiet register of suspected Scarborn, and [[The Unsilvered]] keep a quieter register of the ones who escaped it.

> [!note]- Design Notes
> - **Setting-neutral rules.** No feature depends on the Mirrored Realms; setting lore is kept in an optional section.
> - **Theme split.** Mirrors provide utility (Mirror Sense), space provides mobility (Moment Slip, Fold Space), and time provides control (Stolen Second, Living Scar, Shatter the Hour).
> - **Moment Slip** is limited by spell slots, so it adds mobility without an extra action. Living Scar lets it trigger on cantrips while Innate Sorcery is active.
> - **Stolen Second** forces a reroll rather than letting you choose the result, so it can backfire. At 2 Sorcery Points it competes directly with Metamagic.
> - **Fold Space** helps allies only, so it rewards team positioning rather than forcing enemies around.
> - **Shatter the Hour** follows the pattern of other sorcerer capstones: once per Long Rest, restorable with 5 Sorcery Points.
> - All subclass spells are drawn from the SRD 5.2.1.
> - Playtest focus: whether Dragging Hour is too strong in melee range, whether Stolen Second should be limited per rest, and whether the capstone's Incapacitated effect needs a shorter duration.
