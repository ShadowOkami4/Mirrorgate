# Mirrorbound

> *"Most people glance into a mirror to see whether they look presentable. The Mirrorbound glance into one to check whether they are still standing in it."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

*Fighter Subclass · Fight Alongside Your Own Reflection*

Some warriors learn that a reflection is more than an image. It is a second moment, held in a second place. Mirrorbound fighters walk beside their own reflections, trading places with them and stepping through gaps in time and space that others cannot see.

> [!warning] Playtest Draft
> This subclass is a first draft. Its features may change through playtesting.

## Becoming Mirrorbound

Roll on the table or choose how your reflection came to answer you.

| 1d6 | Origin |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | You touched a strange mirror in an ancient ruin and lost a day you cannot remember. Your reflection remembers it. |
| 2 | You were born on a night when every mirror in the house cracked. Your reflection has always moved a heartbeat before you do. |
| 3 | You trained under a veteran who cast no reflection. When she died, hers came to you. |
| 4 | You survived the touch of a creature made of broken glass. The fragment that struck you never left. |
| 5 | You looked into an heirloom mirror in an old noble house, and something looked back and agreed to follow. |
| 6 | You don't know. One morning your reflection simply stepped aside to let you pass. |

## Level 3: Mirror Double

You can call your reflection out of the glass and into the world.

**Conjure Double.** As a Bonus Action, you conjure your Double—a shimmering reflection of yourself—in an unoccupied space you can see within 15 feet of yourself. You can have only one Double at a time; conjuring a new one destroys the old one.

The Double lasts until it is destroyed, until you dismiss it (no action required), or until you have the Incapacitated condition. It is also destroyed if it is ever more than 30 feet away from you.

**The Double.** Your Double isn't a creature. Its AC equals yours, it has 1 Hit Point and Immunity to all conditions, and creatures can move through its space. If it must make a saving throw, it uses your saving throw bonus for the roll.

**Move Double.** Once on each of your turns, you can move your Double up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see (no action required). It can move through the spaces of creatures.

**Reflection Points.** Some features of this subclass expend Reflection Points. You have a number of Reflection Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You regain 1 expended Reflection Point when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended Reflection Points when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 3: Swap Step

Once on each of your turns, you can teleport, swapping places with your Double. Doing so costs 15 feet of movement.

## Level 3: Mirror Strike

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make one of the attacks as if you occupied your Double's space. Range, reach, cover, and any other part of the attack that depends on your position are determined from the Double's space.

## Level 3: Shattering Reflection

When a creature you can see hits you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction and expend 1 Reflection Point to swap places with your Double. This swap doesn't cost movement. The attack hits the Double instead, you are unaffected by it, and the Double is destroyed.

## Level 7: Flicker of Time

Your reflection lives a heartbeat ahead of you—and, when you need it, a heartbeat behind.

**Early Warning.** You have Advantage on Initiative rolls.

**Rewind.** When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you fails a saving throw, you can take a Reaction and expend 1 Reflection Point to turn back the moment. The creature rerolls the saving throw and must use the new roll.

## Level 10: Backlash

Your reflection no longer breaks quietly.

**Shard Burst.** When Shattering Reflection destroys your Double, the Double explodes into shards. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice), each creature of your choice within 10 feet of the Double's space. *Failure:* 2d8 Force damage. *Success:* Half damage.

**Mirror Reprisal.** When a creature you can see leaves your Double's reach, you can take a Reaction to make an Opportunity Attack against it as if you occupied the Double's space, using your weapon and attack bonus.

## Level 15: Hall of Mirrors

Your reflection has learned to multiply.

**Second Double.** You can have up to two Doubles at the same time. If you conjure a Double while you already have two, one of your choice is destroyed. Move Double can move each of your Doubles.

**Every Reflection.** When you use Swap Step or Shattering Reflection, you choose which Double to swap with. When you use Mirror Strike, you can make one attack from each Double's space, no more than one attack per Double.

**Endless Reflection.** When you roll Initiative and have no Reflection Points remaining, you regain 1 expended Reflection Point.

## Level 18: Paradox

You have stepped through the mirror so often that it no longer quite lets you go.

**Echoing Surge.** When you use Action Surge, each of your Doubles can immediately make one attack with a weapon you are holding against a creature within its reach, using your attack bonus and damage.

**Mirror Realm.** When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can vanish into the mirror instead, along with everything you are wearing and carrying. While vanished, you can't be targeted or affected by anything. At the start of your next turn, you reappear in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of the space you left, with Hit Points equal to twice your Fighter level. Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

## In the Mirrored Realms

> [!info] Optional Setting Lore
> The rules of this subclass work in any fifth-edition world. This section describes how it fits the setting of the Mirrored Realms and can be ignored elsewhere.

Few people choose to become Mirrorbound. Most gain the gift the hard way: by touching a mirror wound in one of the [[Scars of the Primordial War]] and returning—mostly—from [[The Mirrorgate]]. Something of the crossing stays with them: a reflection that lags a moment behind, leads a moment ahead, or occasionally wanders off on its own.

The art is rarely taught in the open. In [[Lustro]], where the crown insists the Mirrorgate does not exist, a soldier who steps out of their own reflection is walking proof of sedition, and the [[Lustral Office]] treats them accordingly. Many Mirrorbound find shelter among [[The Unsilvered]], who have every reason to value a fighter who can be in two places at once.

> [!note]- Design Notes
> - **Setting-neutral rules.** No feature depends on the Mirrored Realms; setting lore is kept in an optional section.
> - **The Double is free; Reflection Points are the limited resource.** Conjuring costs only a Bonus Action, so the subclass's core tricks (Swap Step, Mirror Strike) work every fight. Reflection Points pay for the powerful reactions: Shattering Reflection and Rewind.
> - **30-foot tether.** The Double can't be parked across the battlefield as an escape hatch, and Shattering Reflection can move you at most 30 feet.
> - **Bonus Actions.** Moving the Double needs no action, so only conjuring competes with Second Wind and off-hand attacks.
> - **Swap Step costs 15 feet**, a meaningful share of a 30-foot speed.
> - **Rewind can protect allies**, so it doesn't duplicate the Fighter's Indomitable feature at level 9.
> - Playtest focus: whether Proficiency Bonus is the right number of Reflection Points at levels 3–6, and whether a full negation of a hit (including critical hits) is too strong at level 3.
