# The Unsilvered

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Rebellion is rarely one fire. More often it is a hundred candles hidden from the same wind."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Loose web of anti-crown movements in [[Lustro]]
> - **Opposes** The crown, the [[Blackwood Bloodline]] and the [[Lustral Office]]
> - **Keeps** Proof that [[The Mirrorgate]] exists

## Overview

**The Unsilvered** are not one army or one idea. They are cells, families, scholars, defectors, smugglers and whole villages working against the strict rule of Lustro's crown. Most want some form of freedom restored; what "freedom" means differs sharply between them.

A mirror without its silver backing is only glass: it reflects nothing, and you can see straight through it. The crown first used the word as an insult for people who refused to reflect the kingdom's approved image. The movements took it gladly. Transparency is the whole point.

## Goals

- End censorship and the crown's denial of the Mirrorgate.
- Find the people who disappeared after mirror-related incidents.
- Protect forbidden histories tied to the [[Scars of the Primordial War]].
- Win local freedom from royal law, or more, depending on the cell.

## The Mirrorgate question

The crown says the Mirrorgate does not exist; the Unsilvered keep the proof that it does: witness accounts, forbidden maps, the locations of mirror-scars, lists of the disappeared, smuggled royal documents, objects that age wrongly, and the testimony of people who came back from impossible places. Owning such things is dangerous. Sharing them is worse. The [[Lustral Office]] exists largely to make sure of both.

## Members and allies

- **Cells** that rarely know each other's members.
- **Sympathisers** among scholars, railway workers and border villages.
- **[[Mirrorbound]] and Scarborn** (see [[Scarborn Sorcery]]) who fled the Office's registers.
- **The question of the royal children.** Some cells see every Blackwood as a symbol of oppression. Others believe [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]] could become allies, since they dislike what their parents do. The radicals call this naive: royal blood stays royal, however guilty it feels.

## Hooks

- The party is asked to carry forbidden proof of the Mirrorgate across a checkpoint.
- A cell tries to contact Jacob or Vanessa, and needs a go-between nobody watches.
- A new cell is a crown trap.
- Exposing the truth would bring retaliation down on a village that sheltered the party.
- A rebel archive holds records the Blackwoods thought destroyed.

> [!player] What players know
> "The Unsilvered" is what people in Lustro call the groups that oppose the crown: rebels to some, the only honest people left to others. Being seen with them is enough to get you arrested.
