# Lustral Office

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II, quoting an official of Lustro
> *"No kingdom censors its history. It merely purifies it."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Crown office of records, censorship and enforcement
> - **Serves** The crown of [[Lustro]] and the [[Blackwood Bloodline]]
> - **Armed agents** The Lustral Guard
> - **Hunts** [[The Unsilvered]]

## Overview

The **Lustral Office** is how the crown of [[Lustro]] keeps its registers, approves its histories and enforces its doctrine that [[The Mirrorgate]] does not exist. Its name comes from *lustration*, an old rite of purification. In Lustro the rite is performed on records, testimony and, occasionally, people.

## Goals

- Keep the official history clean: no Mirrorgate, no impossible ruins, no unexplained disappearances.
- Find and silence anyone who keeps proof otherwise.
- Break [[The Unsilvered]].

## Duties

- Census rolls, travel papers, permits and loyalty oaths.
- Approving public histories and school texts.
- Investigating "seditious superstition", above all claims about the Mirrorgate.
- Persuading witnesses to recant, and giving families an official explanation.
- Keeping a quiet register of suspected Scarborn (see [[Scarborn Sorcery]]) and of anyone whose reflection misbehaves (see [[Mirrorbound]]).

## Resources

- **The Lustral Guard**, its armed agents, at every railway station and border crossing.
- **Checks on the trains**, which push smugglers onto old roads like [[The Old Road]].
- **Confiscated evidence**, kept somewhere nobody outside the Office has seen.

> [!secret] What the Office knows
> The Office has seen more proof of the Mirrorgate than anyone in Lustro. Whether its senior officers believe the doctrine they enforce, and what they do with the evidence they seize, is the GM's to decide.

## Hooks

- A Lustral clerk wants out and offers a list of confiscated items in exchange for safe passage.
- The party's travel papers are flagged after they visit the Forest of Thieves.
- An Office inspector arrives in Glasscombe asking about glowing flowers.

> [!gm]- Open questions
> - Who commands the Office? It answers to the crown directly, and so in the end to Queen [[Maria Blackwood]].
> - Where does it keep what it confiscates?

> [!player] What players know
> The Lustral Office keeps the crown's records in Lustro: papers, permits, oaths and approved histories. Its guards check travellers at every station. Talking about the Mirrorgate in public is a good way to meet them.
