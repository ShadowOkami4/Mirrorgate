# Blackwood Bloodline

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"Some families inherit estates. The Blackwoods appear to have inherited consequences."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** The ruling dynasty of [[Lustro]]
> - **Seat** [[Ogledal]], the royal capital
> - **Known for** Strict rule, the crown's propaganda, hidden records
> - **Reigning** Queen [[Maria Blackwood]], with her consort [[Lucien Blackwood]]
> - **Heirs** [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]]

## Overview

The **Blackwoods** are the ruling dynasty of [[Lustro]]; Queen [[Maria Blackwood]] holds the crown by birth. Unlike lesser houses that hold power through wealth or soldiers alone, they are tied to the legitimacy and identity of the realm itself. Their marriages, deaths, scandals and appearances shift alliances across the kingdom; a Blackwood heir is never a private person, only a political event waiting to happen.

The family is strict and deeply invested in public control. It stands behind the crown's doctrine that [[The Mirrorgate]] does not exist, despite the scars, disappearances and contradictions that say otherwise, and the [[Lustral Office]] enforces that doctrine in its name.

## Goals

- Keep the crown's story intact: one kingdom, one history, no Mirrorgate.
- Hold Lustro's borderlands, and the ruins in them, against rival houses.
- Keep the younger generation in line.

## Reputation

- **Loyalists** see them as the rightful royal family.
- **Rival nobles** see dangerous obstacles to their own claims.
- **Common folk** know the name mostly through rumour.
- **Scholars** suspect the house keeps records older than the kingdom.
- **[[The Unsilvered]]** disagree over whether Jacob and Vanessa are allies or liabilities.

> [!secret] Blackwood secrets
> The family's older records are strangely incomplete, and the gaps look deliberate. Possible explanations, for the GM to choose:
> - The family erased parts of its own history.
> - A rival house removed evidence of a Blackwood claim.
> - A war-scar altered the records after the fact.
> - The house protects a secret tied to the founding of Lustro.
> - The crown's denial of the Mirrorgate hides a Blackwood failure, pact or discovery.

## Hooks

- A succession dispute threatens the stability of Lustro.
- A royal archive holds a blank page where the family's origin should be.
- A war-scar recognises Blackwood blood and opens a sealed path.
- Jacob or Vanessa tries to help an anti-crown cell while under surveillance.
- Lucien or Maria orders evidence of the Mirrorgate destroyed, and the party is asked to save it first.

> [!player] What players know
> The Blackwoods are the ruling family of Lustro; Queen Maria Blackwood sits on the throne. They are known for strict rule, and the crown's official line, that the Mirrorgate is a superstition, is theirs. Their two children, Jacob and Vanessa, are rarely seen in public without guards.
