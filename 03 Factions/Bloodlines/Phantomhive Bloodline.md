# Phantomhive Bloodline

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"I assure you, my family is only mostly suspicious."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Family of scholars and minor nobility
> - **Known for** Scholarship, strange inheritances, old houses, and turning up near impossible events
> - **Members** [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] (the original), [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]], Jürgen and Elisabeth Phantomhive

## Overview

The **Phantomhive** name appears in an unusual number of records, from [[Kagameru]] and beyond. In some accounts it behaves less like a surname than an echo, recurring across regions, institutions and even realities that should not share a history. Depending on the source, the family are eccentric academics, minor nobility with major secrets, keepers of forbidden mirrors, patrons of magical science, or a recurring anomaly in Mirrorgate research.

## The recurrence problem

The name keeps coming back where it should not. Some scholars say the [[The Mirrorgate|Mirrorgate]] has altered the family; others think the name is a title, a mantle or a mark placed on certain people by forces nobody understands. The Professor calls these theories "deeply interesting, insufficiently proven, and personally inconvenient".

The evidence:

- **The two Phineases.** When Phineas I was the age Phineas II is now, the two looked nearly identical.
- **Jürgen and Elisabeth, twice.** Phineas I carried his lost uncle Jürgen and aunt Elisabeth with him as other personalities, memories of the family a dragon took from him. Generations later, a different Jürgen and Elisabeth appear in the records, and once again they are the uncle and aunt of a Phineas.
- **Another world's faith.** Phineas I came from a world whose main religion is [[Aurora]], a faith not native to Kagameru.

## Hooks

- A sealed Phantomhive archive opens only for a living family member.
- A mirror shows a Phantomhive ancestor from a reality that never existed.
- Jürgen Phantomhive is found listed among the dead, the living and the not-yet-born.
- The later Elisabeth begins to speak in the voice of the Elisabeth Phineas I remembered.
- A rival scholar claims the bloodline is not a family at all, but a containment system.
