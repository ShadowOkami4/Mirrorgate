# Bloodlines

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A bloodline is a story people insist on telling with crowns."*

The noble and royal bloodlines of [[Kagameru]] preserve fragments of older ages: inheritance records, marriage contracts, curse traditions, divine claims and family myths that may hide real historical power. Not every important bloodline rules a kingdom. Some command academies, monasteries, merchant leagues or monster-hunting orders; some have nothing but a name that refuses to vanish.

| Bloodline | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| [[Blackwood Bloodline]] | The royal house of [[Lustro]]; strict rule, propaganda, missing records |
| [[Phantomhive Bloodline]] | Scholars and a name that keeps recurring across records and worlds |

## Common themes

- Claims of descent from heroes, saints, divine avatars or cursed survivors.
- Secret archives under family estates.
- Inherited magical signs that appear only in a crisis.
- Rival houses competing over ruins, roads and archives touched by the [[Scars of the Primordial War]].
- The same names turning up again and again across [[The Mirrorgate]].
