# Species & Cultures

> *“To study a people by their appearance alone is to judge a library by the color of its door.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The peoples of [[Zerkalo]] are as varied as the lands they inhabit. Across its mirrored realms, ancient lineages stand beside young civilizations, wandering clans cross the borders of settled kingdoms, and communities separated by only a few miles may preserve vastly different customs, beliefs, and histories.

This section records what is known of Zerkalo's intelligent peoples. Each entry explores not only their physical characteristics and innate gifts, but also the many cultures, traditions, and ways of life that have arisen among them.

Species and culture are not the same. Members of a single species may belong to rival nations, speak different languages, worship different gods, and hold little in common beyond distant ancestry. Likewise, one community may include people of many species united by a shared homeland and history.

> [!NOTE]
> These records describe common traditions and historical patterns—not rules that every individual must follow. Adventurers, in particular, have a habit of defying tidy scholarly classifications.
