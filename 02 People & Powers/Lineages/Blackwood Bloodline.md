# Blackwood Bloodline

> *“Some families inherit estates. The Blackwoods appear to have inherited consequences.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The **Blackwood Bloodline** is one of the most important royal families of [[Zerkalo]], known especially for its connection to [[Lustro]]. Unlike lesser noble houses that preserve power through wealth or military service alone, the Blackwoods are tied to the legitimacy, continuity, and political identity of the realm itself.

They are not merely aristocrats. They are a royal house whose name carries authority, expectation, and danger.

## Role in Lustro

The Blackwoods stand close to the center of power in [[Lustro]]. Whether they currently rule directly, serve as the ruling dynasty, or form one of several competing royal branches remains open for later clarification.

What is certain is that their bloodline matters. Their marriages, deaths, scandals, and public appearances can shift alliances across the kingdom. A Blackwood heir is never merely a private person; they are a political event waiting to happen.

The royal side of the family is strict and deeply invested in public control. The crown's propaganda insists that [[The Mirrorgate]] does not exist, despite the many scars, disappearances, and contradictions that suggest otherwise.

Current working records identify [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]] as the parents of [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]]. Jacob and Vanessa disapprove of their parents' actions, but they are watched very carefully.

## Reputation

Public opinion of the Blackwoods varies sharply depending on region, faction, and generation.

Common views include:

- **Loyalists** see them as the rightful royal family of Lustro.
- **Rival nobles** view them as dangerous obstacles to their own claims.
- **Common folk** often know the name more through rumor than direct experience.
- **Scholars** suspect the house preserves records older than the current kingdom.
- **Conspirators** whisper that the Blackwoods know more about the [[Scars of the Primordial War]] than they admit.
- **Resistance groups** disagree over whether Jacob and Vanessa are potential allies or royal liabilities.

## Blackwood Secrets

Older references to the family were strangely incomplete, suggesting missing or damaged records. This absence may now be more suspicious than accidental.

Possible explanations:

- The family deliberately erased parts of its own history.
- A rival house removed evidence of a Blackwood claim.
- The bloodline is connected to a war-scar that altered records retroactively.
- The Blackwoods protect a royal secret tied to the founding of Lustro.
- The family name has appeared in more than one version of history.
- The crown's denial of the Mirrorgate may hide a Blackwood failure, pact, or discovery.
