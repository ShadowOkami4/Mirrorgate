# NPCs

> *“People are the true crossroads of history. Roads merely help them arrive late.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

This folder gathers recurring and adventure-specific NPCs in one place so their relationships remain easy to track through Obsidian links and graph view.

NPCs may belong to a single adventure, a faction, a region, or the wider setting. Keeping them together helps reveal connections between stories, patrons, villains, allies, bloodlines, and unresolved mysteries.

## Organization Notes

- Adventure notes should link to NPCs directly by name.
- NPC notes should link back to relevant adventures, locations, factions, and lore.
- If an NPC appears in more than one adventure, keep one central note here rather than duplicating them inside each adventure folder.
