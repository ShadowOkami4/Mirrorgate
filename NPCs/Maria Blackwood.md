# Maria Blackwood

> *“In royal houses, grace is rarely harmless.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Maria Blackwood** is a known member of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]], an important royal family of [[Lustro]].

## Current Known Facts

- Member of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]].
- Connected to [[Lustro]].
- Parent of [[Jacob Blackwood]] and [[Vanessa Blackwood]] in current working records.
- Associated with the strict royal order of Lustro.
- Supports or benefits from the crown's propaganda denying [[The Mirrorgate]].
- Exact title, age, role, and political position are not yet defined.

## Court Role

Maria is currently framed as part of the parental authority that Jacob and Vanessa quietly oppose. She may be a public face of the royal family, a political strategist, a queenly figure, or someone who understands that propaganda can be sharper than any blade.

## Open Questions

- Is Maria a princess, queen, duchess, regent, diplomat, or hidden claimant?
- What is her exact relationship to [[Lucien Blackwood]]?
- Does she preserve the family's public image because she believes the lie, or because the truth is worse?
- Does she know Jacob and Vanessa disapprove?
