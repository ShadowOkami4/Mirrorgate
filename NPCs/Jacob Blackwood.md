# Jacob Blackwood

> *“The quieter branch of a royal family is often quiet because it is listening.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Jacob Blackwood** is a known member of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]], an important royal family of [[Lustro]].

## Current Known Facts

- Member of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]].
- Connected to [[Lustro]].
- Child of [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]] in current working records.
- Disapproves of his parents' actions.
- Watched very carefully by the crown and royal household.
- Exact title, age, and formal political position are not yet defined.

## Private Conflict

Jacob is caught between blood and conscience. He was raised inside the royal machinery of Lustro, but he does not approve of his parents' actions or the crown's strict propaganda, especially the official denial of [[The Mirrorgate]].

His disapproval is dangerous. Jacob cannot act freely without drawing attention, and anyone who approaches him risks becoming part of the surveillance around him.

## Open Questions

- Is Jacob brave enough to oppose his parents openly?
- Does he already have contact with [[Anti-Crown Movements of Lustro]]?
- How much does he know about the Mirrorgate?
