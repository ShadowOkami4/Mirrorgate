# Thalrex Silberblick

> *“A dragonborn barbarian with wild magic and glowing blue eyes is the sort of detail legends rarely invent politely.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Thalrex Silberblick** is a dragonborn barbarian remembered in legends surrounding [[Phineas Phantomhive I]].

The surviving accounts describe Thalrex as physically formidable, difficult to control, and touched by unstable magic. His eyes are repeatedly described as glowing blue, bright enough that some stories treat them as a warning sign before his magic erupted.

## Known Details

- Associated with [[Phineas Phantomhive I]].
- Traveled or fought alongside [[Dwight]].
- Dragonborn.
- Barbarian or rage-warrior.
- Capable of wild, unpredictable magic.
- Had strangely glowing blue eyes.
- His homeland, final fate, and exact connection to Phineas I are unknown.

## Wild Magic

Thalrex's magic does not appear to have been scholarly or disciplined. Legends suggest it manifested violently, instinctively, or emotionally, especially in battle.

Possible signs of his magic include:

- sudden blue light in his eyes
- uncontrolled elemental bursts
- spells triggered by rage or injury
- mirrorlike fractures in the air around him
- enemies remembering events that did not happen

## Adventure Use

- A dragonborn with glowing blue eyes is mistaken for Thalrex's descendant.
- A weapon associated with Thalrex releases wild magic when used in anger.
- A Mirrorgate reflection shows Thalrex fighting beside Phineas I.
- A ruin from Phineas I's world bears claw marks, scorch marks, and blue afterimages that appear a few seconds before the damage does.
