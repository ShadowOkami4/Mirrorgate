# Vanessa Blackwood

> *“Every royal family has one member everyone underestimates. Sensible historians learn their name first.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Vanessa Blackwood** is a known member of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]], an important royal family of [[Lustro]].

## Current Known Facts

- Member of the [[Blackwood Bloodline]].
- Connected to [[Lustro]].
- Child of [[Lucien Blackwood]] and [[Maria Blackwood]] in current working records.
- Does not like what her parents are doing.
- Watched very carefully by the crown and royal household.
- Exact title, age, and formal political position are not yet defined.

## Private Conflict

Vanessa understands enough of Lustro's court to know when a lie has become policy. She does not approve of her parents' actions or the royal propaganda denying [[The Mirrorgate]], but her position gives her very little room to move openly.

She may be more cautious than rebellious, more observant than loud, and more dangerous because of it. If she chooses to act, she likely does so through hints, intermediaries, and carefully placed omissions.

## Open Questions

- Is Vanessa already helping someone outside the palace?
- Does she trust [[Jacob Blackwood]], or are they watched too closely to speak honestly?
- What proof does she have that the Mirrorgate exists?
