# Dwight

> *“A medic with a rogue's hands is either a blessing, a crime, or both.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Dwight** is a legendary companion associated with [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] in stories from Phineas I's unknown world.

Only fragments survive. Dwight is described as a **war medic rogue**: someone who knew battlefields, wounds, stealth, and survival. Whether he was formally trained as a healer, a battlefield scavenger with a conscience, or something stranger remains unknown.

## Known Details

- Associated with [[Phineas Phantomhive I]].
- Remembered as a war medic and rogue.
- Appears in legends alongside [[Thalrex Silberblick]].
- His full name, homeland, species, and fate are unknown.

## Possible Interpretations

- A battlefield surgeon who used rogue skills to reach the wounded.
- A thief pressed into war who learned medicine out of necessity.
- A spy whose cover as a medic became a genuine calling.
- A survivor whose knowledge of death made him invaluable to heroes.

## Adventure Use

- A bloodstained medical kit bears Dwight's name.
- A coded field journal records Phineas I's travels.
- A healing technique from Dwight's world works on wounds that Zerkalo magic cannot mend.
- Someone claims Dwight betrayed Phineas, but the evidence is incomplete.
