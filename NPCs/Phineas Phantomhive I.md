# Phineas Phantomhive I

> *“A name may cross worlds more easily than a man. Unfortunately, in this case, both appear to have done so.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

**Phineas Phantomhive I**, often called the **Original Phineas**, is one of the strangest figures connected to the [[Phantomhive Bloodline]]. He is not known to have originated in [[Zerkalo]]. Current records agree only that he came from an unknown world beyond the present one.

Almost everything else about him remains uncertain.

## Known Facts

- Phineas Phantomhive I came from a different world.
- The name, condition, and fate of his family are unknown.
- The circumstances that brought him away from his home world are unknown.
- When Phineas I was the same apparent age as [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]] is now, the two looked remarkably similar.
- The most reliable surviving cultural detail from his world concerns its main religion: the worship of [[Aurora]].

## Resemblance to Phineas II

The resemblance between Phineas Phantomhive I and [[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]] is difficult to dismiss. Records describe the first Phineas, at the relevant age, as nearly identical in appearance to the second.

This similarity may indicate ordinary inheritance, Mirrorgate distortion, a repeated soul-pattern, deliberate imitation, or something stranger. No conclusion is currently safe.

> [!question] Unresolved Mystery
> Was Phineas II named after the first because of family memory, or does the name itself return when certain conditions are met?

## Unknown Family Fate

The fate of Phineas I's family is one of the largest gaps in the Phantomhive records. It is not known whether they died, remained in their original world, crossed into another reality, or were erased from surviving accounts.

This absence should be treated as important rather than empty. Someone may have failed to preserve the truth, or someone may have worked very hard to remove it.

## The World He Left Behind

The original world of Phineas Phantomhive I has no confirmed name in current records. Its geography, politics, and history remain mostly unknown.

The one major surviving detail is religious: its main faith centered on [[Aurora]], a genderless deity of life. This is a fact about **Phineas I's world**, not about Zerkalo.

> [!important]
> The faith of Aurora is **not** the main religion of [[Zerkalo]]. It is the main religion of the unknown world associated with Phineas Phantomhive I.

## Heroic Legends

Some legends from beyond Zerkalo describe Phineas Phantomhive I as a hero. These accounts are fragmentary and cannot yet be verified, but they consistently place him in the company of two unusual companions.

The first companion is remembered only as [[Dwight]], a war medic and rogue. The contradiction in that description appears in several tellings: a battlefield healer who moved like a thief, or perhaps a thief who learned to keep people alive because the war gave him no better choice.

The second companion is named more fully: [[Thalrex Silberblick]], a dragonborn barbarian with strangely glowing blue eyes. Legends claim Thalrex could cast magic wildly, unpredictably, and without the discipline expected of trained mages.

The stories disagree on what the three fought, where they traveled, and whether their deeds ended in triumph, exile, or disaster. Still, the repeated grouping of Phineas, Dwight, and Thalrex suggests that these were not isolated names.

> [!note] Reliability
> These are legends, not confirmed biography. They should be treated as true enough to matter, but incomplete enough to become dangerous.

## Adventure Use

- A portrait of Phineas I is mistaken for Phineas II.
- A religious artifact of [[Aurora]] reacts to the Phantomhive name.
- A mirror shows Phineas I's family, but no one in the reflection recognizes Zerkalo.
- Records about Phineas I have been deliberately altered to hide what happened to his home world.
- A battlefield song names Phineas, [[Dwight]], and [[Thalrex Silberblick]] as heroes of a war no Zerkalo historian recognizes.
- Thalrex's glowing blue eyes appear in a vision when wild magic tears open a mirror.
