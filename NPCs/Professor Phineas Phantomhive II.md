# Professor Phineas Phantomhive II

> *“If the truth refuses to sit still, one must either chase it or become a librarian. I chose, regrettably, both.”*

**Professor Phineas Phantomhive II** is the chief chronicler of the **Encyclopedia Kagami** and the principal scholarly voice behind the [[Welcome|Library of Kagami]]. He is a traveler, lecturer, collector of contradictory histories, and member of the [[Phantomhive Bloodline]].

## Role in the Setting

Professor Phineas serves as an in-world guide to the scattered records of [[Zerkalo]]. His notes often appear at the beginning of entries, offering dry commentary, warnings, or scholarly corrections that may or may not be as objective as he believes.

He began his work in [[Hansha]], convinced that the world could be catalogued cleanly. Years of travel through ruins, courts, monster-haunted roads, and mirror-touched places corrected this optimism.

## Scholarly Interests

- the lost histories of [[The Primordial War]]
- temporal and spatial wounds connected to the [[Scars of the Primordial War]]
- the recurring mystery of [[The Mirrorgate]]
- noble houses and bloodlines, especially his own inconvenient [[Phantomhive Bloodline]]
- species, cultures, and the ways scholars misunderstand both

## Known Family Records

The Phantomhive records currently name:

- [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] — the original Phineas, apparently from another world.
- **Phineas Phantomhive II**
- **Jürgen Phantomhive**

The exact relation between these names requires further documentation.

The first and second Phineas are known to have looked remarkably similar when Phineas I was at the same apparent age that Phineas II is now. Whether this is simple inheritance, Mirrorgate recurrence, or something stranger remains unresolved.

## Using the Professor

Phineas can appear as:

- a narrator for setting entries
- a quest giver seeking lost records
- a patron of expeditions
- a comic academic foil
- a suspiciously well-informed survivor of events he should not remember
