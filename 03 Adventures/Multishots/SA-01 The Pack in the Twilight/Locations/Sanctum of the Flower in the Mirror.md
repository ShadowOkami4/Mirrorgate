# Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror

> *"There are places reflected in mirrors, and there are places that wait behind them. Confusing the two is how expeditions become memorials."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The **Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror**, also called *the temple behind the mirror*, is the missing half of the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]. It hangs on a shard of land in the Drift of [[The Mirrorgate]], surrounded by the reflections of the forest it was taken from.

Almost everything in the Mirrorgate is a copy: a reflection of somewhere else, reversed and imperfect. The sanctum is not. Its stones are the real stones that vanished from the ruin in the [[Forest of Thieves]], and that makes it one of the strangest places behind the glass.

## The Name

Its lintel carries the half of the old saying that is missing from the ruin in Zerkalo: *the flower in the mirror*. Read together, the two inscriptions form the complete saying—*the flower in the mirror, the moon in the water*—a description of things that can be seen but never grasped.

In a world where every reflected sign reads backward, this inscription reads the right way round. Travelers who notice have their first proof that the sanctum is not a reflection.

## The Shard

The sanctum's shard is visible from far across the Drift because of the water. The still pool in the temple's courtyard has reflected the ruin for centuries, and its reflection has become a pool on the shard. That pool spills over the shard's edge and falls *upward* in a slow silver column toward a larger shard above.

Beneath the shard hangs an upside-down forest: the reflection of the trees around the ruin, reflected in the pool and grown down from the underside of the rock. Travelers who arrive through the pool often find themselves among these inverted trees first, climbing *up* toward the temple.

## Two Halves on One Shard

The shard holds two different kinds of place, joined along a visible seam.

- **The reflected ruin.** Most of the shard is a deep reflection of the Temple of the Moon in Water as it stands today, created by the pool's long mirror memory. It is faithful but reversed: broken columns lean the wrong way, moss grows on the wrong side of the stones, and the courtyard mirrors the ruin down to its fallen leaves.
- **The true sanctum.** At the heart of the shard stand the chambers that are missing from the ruin in Zerkalo—the rooms behind doorways that open onto nothing, and the walls whose outlines are all that remain on the other side. These are not reversed. They are the originals.
- **The seam.** Where the two halves meet, reversed and unreversed stonework are fused along a jagged line, like a fracture that healed badly. [[Life in the Mirrorgate|Seam moss]] glows along its entire length.

## Time in the Sanctum

The reflected ruin keeps time with the forest outside the real temple: an afternoon there is an afternoon in Zerkalo.

The true sanctum does not. Cut loose from its original place, its time has come apart, and each chamber may belong to a different moment: untouched stone, active worship, fresh destruction, and ancient ruin can stand side by side. A doorway beside the altar might open back onto the physical ruin centuries—or only seconds—away from the moment the traveler left.

## Life Around the Sanctum

The shard is not deserted. Lanternweed drifts around its edges, silverhorns graze in the reflected courtyard, and upfall salmon leap up the rising waterfall. These natives are wary but not hostile; see [[Life in the Mirrorgate]].

Where the true sanctum's time grows most unstable, the natives stay away—and [[Shardkin]] may gather instead.

## Current Adventure Role

- the missing half of the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]], and the likely goal of the party's journey north
- the characters' first experience of the Mirrorgate, with its shards, the Drift, and reversed reflections
- a location tied to the planar disturbance in the Forest of Thieves
- the place connected to [[Maria Rose]] and her investigation

## Maria Rose

[[Maria Rose]] is connected to the temple through her investigation of the luminous plants and the planar disturbance. Her nature as a ghost may be related to the true sanctum's broken time, but the cause and rules of that connection remain undefined.

Possible directions include:

- Maria is caught in one of the sanctum's loose moments, repeating the hour in which something happened to her.
- Maria crossed through the pool and her body remained on one side while her spirit remained on the other.
- Maria is exactly where she means to be, guarding something in the sanctum until help arrives.
