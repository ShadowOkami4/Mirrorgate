# Temple of the Moon in Water

> *“A ruin is only an abandoned place when nothing on the other side remembers it.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The **Temple of the Moon in Water**—to most who know of it, simply *the ruined temple*—stands in the northern reaches of the [[Forest of Thieves]]. It is the site marked on the map given to the adventurers by [[Sandra Mistic]] and the place where her old friend, [[Maria Rose]], investigated the spread of the luminous plants.

The ruin exists within [[Zerkalo]], but it is not confined to Zerkalo. Somewhere beyond its broken surfaces and displaced reflections, the same temple continues inside [[The Mirrorgate]] as [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]].

These are not simply two temples built to resemble one another. They are two manifestations of one place divided across reality: a physical ruin in Zerkalo and a temple caught within the plane between time and space.

## The Name

The temple's name survives on a cracked lintel above its entrance as half of an old saying: *the moon in the water*. The complete saying—*the flower in the mirror, the moon in the water*—describes things that can be seen but never grasped. The missing half of the inscription was carved on the part of the temple that is no longer here: [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]].

A character who reads the inscription and succeeds on a **DC 14 Intelligence (History or Religion) check** recognizes the saying and realizes that its other half is missing.

## Current Adventure Role

- northern destination shown on Sandra's map
- last established location connected to Maria Rose's investigation
- site where the luminous plants and the planar disturbance overlap
- physical counterpart of [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]]
- potential threshold into [[The Mirrorgate]], through its courtyard pool or its missing mirror

## The Divided Temple

The Temple of the Moon in Water is the portion of the location that remains anchored to ordinary geography. Its stones can be touched, mapped, and approached through the forest, but its structure is incomplete in ways that collapse alone cannot explain.

A doorway may have no chamber behind it. A wall preserves the outline of a mirror that is no longer present. Portions of the temple appear to have been removed cleanly rather than destroyed. The missing architecture now stands on the other side of reality as the [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]]: not a reflection of the temple, but its missing half.

## The Courtyard Pool

At the temple's heart lies a still, dark pool that has reflected the ruin for centuries. On calm nights, the pool shows something that is not there: a slow column of silver water falling *upward* into the sky. This is the reflection of the sanctum's shard in [[The Mirrorgate]].

A character who watches the pool at night and succeeds on a **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check** notices the rising waterfall in the reflection.

The relationship between the two temples should remain stronger than an ordinary portal. Damage, movement, or change on one side may be reflected imperfectly on the other, although the exact rules have not yet been established.

## Present Condition

The temple is old, broken, and partially reclaimed by the forest. Its final layout, original faith, and surviving chambers remain open for later development.

The following elements are established:

- it stands north of [[To the Eternal Lady]] within the Forest of Thieves
- Sandra considers it connected to the current planar disturbance
- Maria Rose investigated the unnatural plants near the site
- some part of the temple exists inside the Mirrorgate
- its ruins may provide a passage to that mirrored location
