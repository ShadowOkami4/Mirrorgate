# Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror

> *"There are places reflected in mirrors, and there are places that wait behind them. Confusing the two is how expeditions become memorials."*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The **Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror**, also called *the temple behind the mirror*, is the missing half of the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]. Together, the two halves were once the **Shrine of the Unreachable Bloom**, a shrine to a moon god destroyed in [[The Primordial War]].

The sanctum lies inside [[The Mirrorgate]], but it does not behave like the Mirrorgate. Almost everything behind the glass is a reflection of somewhere else. The sanctum is not. Its stones are the real stones that vanished from the ruin in the [[Forest of Thieves]].

## How the Sanctum Came to Be

The shrine's priests and clerics tried to revive their dead god with a great ritual. It failed drastically. A rift opened and swallowed half of the shrine, and that half has been inside the Mirrorgate ever since.

The rift's power was absorbed into the **great mirror** in the temple's main hall. That is why the mirror still shows the sanctum, and why stepping through it leads there. See the [[Temple of the Moon in Water#The Great Mirror|Great Mirror]].

## Disconnected

Because the sanctum was torn into the Mirrorgate rather than reflected there, it is one of the only places in the plane that is not a reflection, and it is strangely **disconnected** from every other part of the Mirrorgate. No path across the Drift leads to it. As far as anyone knows, the only way in is through the great mirror.

## The Name

Its lintel carries the half of the old saying that is missing from the ruin in Kagameru: *the flower in the mirror*. Read together, the two inscriptions form the complete saying—*the flower in the mirror, the moon in the water*—a description of things that can be seen but never grasped.

In a world where every reflected sign reads backward, this inscription reads the right way round. Travelers who notice have their first proof that the sanctum is not a reflection.

## A Bizarre Place

The sanctum is bizarre even by the standards of the Mirrorgate.

**Frozen in time.** Most of the sanctum is extremely well kept, as if it had been frozen in time on the night of the ritual. Floors are swept, hangings are whole, and offerings still lie on the altars. Unlike the ruin in the forest, nothing here has decayed.

**The bleeding edge.** In other parts, the Mirrorgate is slowly bleeding through. Plants from the Mirrorgate grow through the walls and floors, breaking the sanctum apart—but only up to a certain point. Beyond it, they stop, as if an unknown power were holding them back.

**The outer ring.** Where the Mirrorgate has broken through, the sanctum's outer ring is home to wild fauna from behind the glass (see [[Life in the Mirrorgate]]).

> [!question] Open Mystery
> What power holds the Mirrorgate back from the rest of the sanctum?

## Puzzles

Like the temple, the sanctum is full of puzzles left by the shrine's builders. The party must solve them to make its way inward to the great hall. The sanctum is explored in [[SA-01 Chapter 3]].

## The Great Hall

At the heart of the sanctum lies the **great hall**, where the ritual to revive the moon god took place. This is where the party finds [[Maria Rose]].

### Meeting Maria

Maria tells the party what happened to the shrine: the failed ritual and the rift that tore the sanctum away. She also warns them that a **strange, evil power looms in the shrine**.

**If the party found the clues.** Characters who uncovered the diaries and handbook entries in the temple and sanctum already know much of the story. Maria's account confirms and completes what they pieced together.

**If the party missed the clues.** Characters who did not find the diaries and handbook entries are surprised and ask what she means by "the shrine." Maria explains its history in full. Missing the clues does not lock the party out of the story; they simply learn it from her instead of discovering it themselves.

**Her research.** Maria then shares what she found while exploring the sanctum and studying the wild fauna of its outer ring.

> [!warning] Story Beat: The Red Fog
> The red fog rises during this conversation and corrupts Maria. See [[SA-01 Chapter 3#The Red Fog]].
