*A multishot adventure for characters of 4th level.*

## Adventure Overview

Strangers from distant roads each receive the same letter, asking them to come to an inn hidden in the [[Forest of Thieves]]. They meet at twilight, survive a band of frightened thieves, and reach [[To the Eternal Lady]], where the innkeeper [[Sandra Mistic]] reveals that she is a lich bound to guard a dangerous artifact.

Sandra cannot leave the inn. Her old friend [[Maria Rose]] sent a plea for help from a temple in the north, where strange luminous plants have begun to spread. Sandra asks the adventurers to go in her place.

The temple is not entirely in Kagameru. The [[Temple of the Moon in Water]] in the forest is only one half of a place divided by [[The Mirrorgate]]; its other half is [[Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror]]. And somewhere in the twilight between the trees, something is hunting the party.

## Adventure Background

> [!gm] What the GM Knows
> - Sandra's artifact reacted to each adventurer before she sent the letters. She does not know whether it chose them as protectors, liberators, or victims.
> - The luminous plants grow from glasslike cracks in the earth. They are not native to Kagameru—or perhaps to any plane.
> - Maria Rose is a ghost. How she became one, and where she currently exists between the two temples, is not yet defined.
> - Sandra's wards are weakening faster than she admits. She suspects the disturbance is not trying to enter Kagameru, but to retrieve something already here.
> - Large wolf tracks circle the party. They vanish for several paces at a time, as though the creature passes through shadow itself.

## Running the Adventure

- **Rules:** fifth-edition rules using the SRD 5.2.1. Creature stat blocks use the current format.
- **Structure:** chapters are played in order. Each chapter note contains read-aloud text, checks, and encounters; characters and locations are kept in their own notes and referenced from the chapters.
- **Read-aloud text** appears in quote callouts. GM-only information appears in callouts marked GM.
