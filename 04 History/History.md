# History

Eras and events as they really happened, oldest first.

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"History is what survives the fire. Myth is what people carry out of it."*

The history of [[Kagameru]] is not one clean road. It is a shelf of damaged maps, contradictory testimonies, temple songs, royal propaganda and field notes rescued from ruins that should not have survived. Many entries here are **scholarly reconstructions**, not certain truth: different cultures keep different names, motives and endings for the same event.
