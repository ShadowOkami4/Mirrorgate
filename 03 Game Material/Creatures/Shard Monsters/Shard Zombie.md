# Shard Zombie

*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*

> A corpse held upright by glasslike force. Its soul is gone; something jagged moves in the empty space.

---

**Armor Class** 12 (shard plating)  
**Hit Points** 22 (3d8 + 9)  
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 16 (+3) | 3 (-4) | 6 (-2) | 5 (-3) |

**Damage Resistances** necrotic  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8  
**Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but cannot speak  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  

---

## Traits

**Shattering Death.** When the shard zombie drops to 0 hit points, it explodes into glasslike shards. Each creature within 5 feet must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 5 (2d4) slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Weak Reflection (1/Day).** As a bonus action, the shard zombie creates a single dim duplicate of itself for 1 minute. When a creature attacks the zombie, roll a d20. On an 11 or higher, the attack hits the duplicate instead, destroying it.

## Actions

**Shard Arm.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing or bludgeoning damage.
