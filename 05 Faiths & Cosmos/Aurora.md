# Aurora

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A foreign god should not be made native merely because their story is beautiful."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Deity of** Life
> - **Worshipped in** The unnamed world [[Phineas Phantomhive I]] came from, **not** in [[Kagameru]]
> - **Shown as** A serpent with shining scales, sharp teeth, watchful eyes and healing venom
> - **Pronouns** Aurora has no gender; the faith says "they", though outsiders often say "goddess"

## Overview

**Aurora** is a genderless deity of life, the central figure of the main religion in the world Phineas Phantomhive I came from. Aurora's followers do not deny that other gods exist; they hold Aurora to be the most important and the most powerful. Tradition says Aurora once descended on a continent, later named in their honour, and brought life to the world.

> [!important] An otherworld faith
> Aurora is **not** a god of Kagameru, and their faith is **not** a religion of the Mirrored Realms. What is known comes from the legends of Phineas I and from one traveller who crossed into Aurora's world through [[The Mirrorgate]] and came back.

## Worship

- **A faith of life in a world of final death.** Resurrection exists in Aurora's world but is difficult, costly and rare. A religion of life carries particular weight where lives cannot easily be restored.
- **A god who answers.** Aurora is believed to intervene directly in dark times: rarely, but unmistakably. The old accounts tell of a paladin granted several years of divine immortality, of Aurora's artifacts sent into the mortal world, and of action whenever the survival of life itself was threatened.
- **The serpent.** In sacred art Aurora is a serpent whose venom heals: dangerous and life-giving at once.

## The world of Aurora

- **Name** Unknown in Kagameru's records.
- **Death** Rarely undone.
- **Syrid** The traveller brought back word of a crystal-like substance called *Syrid*. Nothing more is known.

## Hooks

- A relic of Aurora heals a dying creature, but leaves it with serpent's eyes.
- A paladin claims to carry Aurora's immortality, for a limited number of years.
- An artifact from Phineas I's world appears in Kagameru and behaves unlike any local relic.
- A cult mistakes Aurora for a god of Kagameru, with dangerous results.

> [!player] What players know
> Aurora is a god of life from another world, known in Kagameru only from the old stories of Phineas Phantomhive I. Their followers picture them as a serpent whose venom heals.
