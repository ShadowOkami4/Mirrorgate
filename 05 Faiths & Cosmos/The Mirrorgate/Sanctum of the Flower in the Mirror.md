# Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror

> [!quote] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"There are places reflected in mirrors, and there are places that wait behind them. Confusing the two is how expeditions become memorials."*

> [!location] At a glance
> - **Kind** The torn-away half of a shrine, inside [[The Mirrorgate]]
> - **Other half** The [[Temple of the Moon in Water]], in the [[Forest of Thieves]]
> - **Way in** The great mirror in the temple's worship hall; no other known route
> - **Who is here** [[Maria Rose]], in the great hall

## Overview

The **Sanctum of the Flower in the Mirror**, also called *the temple behind the mirror*, is the missing half of the [[Temple of the Moon in Water]]. Together the two halves were the **Shrine of the Unreachable Bloom**, built for a moon god destroyed in [[The Primordial War]].

The sanctum lies inside the Mirrorgate but does not behave like it. Almost everything behind the glass is a reflection of somewhere else; the sanctum is not. Its stones are the real stones that vanished from the ruin in the forest.

## How the sanctum came to be

The shrine's priests tried to revive their dead god with a great ritual. It failed, and a rift swallowed half of the shrine into the Mirrorgate. The rift's power was absorbed into the great mirror in the temple's worship hall, which is why the mirror still shows the sanctum and why stepping through it leads here. See [[Temple of the Moon in Water#The great mirror]].

## Disconnected

Because the sanctum was torn into the Mirrorgate rather than reflected there, it is cut off from the rest of the plane. No path across the Drift leads to it; as far as anyone knows, the great mirror is the only way in.

## The name

The sanctum's lintel carries the half of the old saying missing from the ruin: *the flower in the mirror*. Read together, the two inscriptions form the whole saying, *the flower in the mirror, the moon in the water*. In a world where every reflected sign reads backward, this one reads the right way round: the party's first proof that the sanctum is not a reflection.

## A bizarre place

- **Frozen in time.** Most of the sanctum is perfectly kept, as if frozen on the night of the ritual. Floors are swept, hangings are whole, and offerings still lie on the altars.
- **The bleeding edge.** Elsewhere the Mirrorgate is slowly bleeding through. Plants from behind the glass grow through walls and floors, breaking the sanctum apart, but only up to a certain line. Beyond it they stop, as if something is holding them back.
- **The outer ring.** Where the Mirrorgate has broken through, wild creatures from behind the glass have moved in (see [[Life in the Mirrorgate]]).

Like the temple, the sanctum is full of the builders' puzzles; the party must solve them to reach the great hall. The sanctum is explored in [[SA-01 Chapter 3]].

## The great hall

At the heart of the sanctum is the great hall, where the ritual took place. Here the party finds [[Maria Rose]].

- **Maria's story.** She tells what happened to the shrine and warns that a strange, evil power looms in it. Characters who found the diaries in the temple and sanctum hear their findings confirmed; characters who missed them learn the whole story from her instead.
- **Her research.** She shares what she learned studying the creatures of the outer ring.

> [!danger] Story beat: the red fog
> The red fog rises during this conversation and corrupts Maria. See [[SA-01 Chapter 3#The Red Fog]].
