# The Lustral Rite

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"In Lustro, to purify is a sacrament, a duty, and a department. Visitors are advised not to confuse the three, because the officials certainly do not."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** The official faith of [[Lustro]], centred on the crown
> - **Seat** [[The Veiled Cathedral]] in [[Ogledal]]
> - **Head** The reigning monarch, Queen [[Maria Blackwood]], crowned as the realm's Purifier
> - **Teaches** Order, cleanliness of body and record, and obedience to the crown that keeps the realm pure

## Overview

**The Lustral Rite** is the faith of the crown of Lustro. Its name and its central sacrament come from *lustration*, the old rite of purification by water and ceremony. The Rite teaches that the realm, like a body, must be kept clean, and that the monarch is its **Purifier**: crowned in a lustration beneath the dome of the Veiled Cathedral, and charged from that day to keep the kingdom's laws, records and memory free of corruption.

The Rite does not deny the gods. It simply places them far away, and the crown very close.

## Worship

- **Lustration.** The faithful are washed at birth, at marriage and before death. Each washing is recorded by a clerk, so that every life in Lustro begins and ends in a register.
- **The veils.** During every service, the mirrors and polished surfaces of a church are covered. Officially this keeps the faithful from vanity. In the Rite's teaching, only the Purifier may look into the cathedral's mirrors, because only the crown may *survey*. It is one of the old meanings of *lustro*.
- **The coronation.** A new monarch is washed, veiled, and then unveiled before a single great mirror behind the altar. What the monarch sees in it is never recorded.
- **Purity of record.** Correcting a record is a pious act. Spreading a false account, such as tales of [[The Mirrorgate]], is a sin against the realm as well as a crime.

## The Rite and the crown

The Rite, the crown and the [[Lustral Office]] are three faces of one idea. The cathedral blesses the registers, the Office keeps them, and the crown is the Purifier of both. Priests of the Rite sit beside Lustral officials at inquiries, and a confession to one is often read by the other.

## The truth

> [!secret] The great mirror behind the altar
> The coronation mirror is a deep reflection, centuries old, and its other side lies in [[The Mirrorgate]]. Every monarch has looked into it. The GM decides what they saw, and whether it is the reason the crown denies the Mirrorgate so fiercely.

## Hooks

- A cleric of the Rite asks the party to "purify" a village register, and the corrections are lies.
- A priest who saw something in an unveiled mirror is quietly reassigned to a distant parish; the party is asked to find out why.
- At a royal wedding in the Veiled Cathedral, the veil over the great mirror is drawn back too early.

> [!player] What players know
> The Lustral Rite is Lustro's official faith. Its followers are washed at birth, marriage and death, and the monarch is crowned as the realm's "Purifier" in the Veiled Cathedral, where every mirror is veiled during prayer.
