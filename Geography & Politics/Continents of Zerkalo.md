# Continents of Zerkalo

> *“One must be careful with old maps. They are often correct about mountains and terribly wrong about everything important.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The surviving atlas fragments of [[Zerkalo]] preserve several continental names. Some may be ancient, some may be regional, and some may be errors copied so often that they became tradition.

For now, the following names are kept as working references until fuller regional histories are established.

## Provisional Continental Names

| Name | Current Notes |
|---|---|
| **Eldorwyn** | A name associated in older records with old forests, noble settlements, and early magical traditions. |
| **Noctheris** | Appears in darker travel accounts and may refer to a region of long nights, shadowed coasts, or lands touched by planar distortion. |
| **Sylvarion** | Usually linked to green realms, ancient groves, and cultures with strong ties to nature or fey-touched magic. |
| **Avaloria** | Often treated as a heartland name in surviving political fragments, possibly tied to knightly houses or old kingdoms. |
| **Frosthavn** | A northern name associated with cold seas, hard winters, and isolated holds. |
| **Azcotal** | A southern or sun-scorched name appearing near references to temples, stone cities, and older rites. |

> [!warning] Provisional Canon
> These names come from legacy notes and should be considered editable. They are preserved here so they can be reviewed, revised, renamed, or discarded without losing the original ideas.

## Tensions Across the Map

Even incomplete records show recurring causes of conflict:

- competition for ruins caught in broken time
- noble claims based on half-remembered bloodlines
- religious disputes over visible scars of [[The Primordial War]]
- expeditions vanishing near mirror-linked sites
- border conflicts sparked by newly uncovered ruins
