# Geography & Politics

> *“Maps are arguments drawn in ink.”*  
> —[[Professor Phineas Phantomhive II]]

The lands of [[Zerkalo]] are divided not only by mountains, rivers, and seas, but by memory, ambition, trade, faith, and old wounds left by powers no living cartographer fully understands.

This section gathers notes on regions, borders, rulers, conflicts, and the political forces that shape the Mirrored Realms.

> [!NOTE]
> Several surviving maps disagree with one another. Until the atlas is properly revised, some names should be treated as provisional scholarly labels rather than final canon.

## Political Themes

- Rival powers competing over access to [[Scars of the Primordial War]]
- Noble houses preserving partial records of pre-modern history
- Borderlands where impossible ruins matter more than capitals
- Scholars, churches, and rulers disagreeing over who has the right to study ancient war-scars
- Hidden routes connected to [[The Mirrorgate]]
- Crown propaganda in [[Lustro]] denying that the Mirrorgate exists
- Organizations working against royal control to regain freedom
