# Counterfeit Sovereign

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"The most dangerous counterfeit is not the one that replaced a king. It is the one that has done the job better than he did, and knows it."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Medium Aberration (Shapechanger) · CR 9 (5,000 XP) · **Boss**
> - **Found** On thrones, in councils, at the head of old families; always near a great mirror
> - **Copies** A ruler, and everyone that ruler has ever looked at
> - **Weak to** The truth, said out loud in front of witnesses; and the mirror it came from
> - **Stronger than** the [[Counterfeit Duelist]] and the [[Counterfeit]]

## Lore

A **counterfeit sovereign** stepped out of a mirror in a great house and never went back. It has worn its original's face for years, sometimes decades. It has signed their laws, raised their children and buried their friends, and it has collected every face it ever needed along the way. It no longer wants to replace one person; it wants to *be* the house, and it is very good at it.

> [!secret] Where the original is
> Most sovereigns keep their original alive: locked in a cellar, a tower or the Mirrorgate itself, because the reflection still needs someone to reflect. Freeing the original weakens the sovereign: it loses Legendary Resistance until it finds them again.

## How it plays

> [!combat] Running the sovereign
> - **Talk first.** It has every reason to bargain, bribe and blame someone else.
> - **Fight in its hall.** Hall of Mirrors makes it hard to ambush; Glass Step lets it vanish between the mirrors.
> - **Turn the party.** Mirror Image, Which Is Which? and Steal a Face are about confusion, not damage.
> - **Use Mirrored Statistics** with the guards and courtiers it has copied: its AC and weapons change as it changes faces.
>
> **GM tip.** Before the fight, let it spend a whole scene as a friendly NPC the party already likes.

## Hooks

- A beloved duke has ruled kindly for twenty years; the portrait in his gallery shows his rings on the wrong hand.
- Queen [[Maria Blackwood]]'s coronation mirror in [[The Veiled Cathedral]] has never been unveiled since her crowning. Nobody knows why.
- A noble family asks the party to find out why their father has started answering to a different name in his sleep.

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> A tall mirror frame of black glass, worth 1,500 gp to a collector, that shows every face the sovereign ever wore, one after another. Destroying the frame frees anyone it kept imprisoned in the Mirrorgate.
