# Glass Husk

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"The body remembers how to walk long after it has forgotten why."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Medium Undead · CR 1/2 (100 XP)
> - **Found** Where a person broke: bedrooms, halls of mirrors, ruined shrines
> - **Often with** [[Glint|Glints]] and a [[Remnant]]: the rest of the same person
> - **Weak to** Reach and spells; it bursts when it dies
> - **Tell** The sound of a mirror being scratched

## Lore

A **glass husk** is the body left behind when a person is shattered by a mirror. The soul is gone; in its place, something jagged has moved in. Thin plates of glass push through the skin like frost across a windowpane, and the husk's joints grind with the sound of a mirror being scratched.

Husks are slow, stubborn, and single-minded. They often return to the place where they broke—a bedroom, a hall of mirrors, a ruined shrine—and stand before the glass as if waiting for it to give something back. When destroyed, a husk bursts apart in a spray of razor-edged fragments.

## Signs it is near

- A body standing perfectly still in front of a mirror, facing the glass.
- Frost-like patterns on windows in a room with no cold.
- Footprints that circle the same spot hundreds of times.

## What characters know

> [!rule] Lore checks
> A character who sees the glass husk can make a check; a higher result also reveals everything below it.
> - **Intelligence (Religion or Arcana) DC 10:** It is not a zombie: no necromancy holds it up. Something like glass moved in where the soul was.
> - **Intelligence (Religion or Arcana) DC 15:** It bursts into razor-edged glass when destroyed; fight it from reach.
> - **Intelligence (Religion or Arcana) DC 20:** Its glints and remnant are nearby. Find a name, and the remnant may hesitate.

## In combat

> [!combat] Running the glass husk
> - Walks straight at the nearest living creature and keeps swinging.
> - Uses Faint Reflection on its first turn, so the first hits often shatter the duplicate.
> - Never retreats; it is already where it wants to be.
>
> **GM tip.** Its bursting death rewards fighting at reach or with spells, and punishes anyone who crowds around it.

## Variants

- **Gilded husk.** A noble's husk still wearing its jewellery: AC 14, and its Burst of Glass deals 7 (2d6) Slashing damage.
- **Shrine husk.** A priest's husk that kneels when a moon is reflected in water nearby, and has the Incapacitated condition while it does.

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> Its glass plates are worth 1d6 × 5 gp to a glazier who doesn't ask questions. One plate in ten still shows the face of the person it was.

## Hooks

- A family asks the party to bring home their missing son; the husk in the old manor is wearing his ring.
- A glazier's best mirrors are made from husk glass, and they have started showing faces.

> [!rumor] What people say
> In old houses, people say, sometimes a body is found standing in front of a mirror, and it does not fall down when you push it. It turns around.
