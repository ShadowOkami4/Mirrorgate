# Kaleidoscope

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"In each shard I saw a different face, and every one of them had once stood where I was standing. I left before it could add mine."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Large Aberration · CR 6 (2,300 XP)
> - **Found** In rooms full of broken mirrors: abandoned halls, collapsed palaces, careless enchanters' workshops
> - **Often with** [[Afterimage|Afterimages]] and [[Counterfeit|counterfeits]] it has drawn in
> - **Weak to** Bludgeoning damage: the glass cracks loudly
> - **Tell** A chiming like wind bells, and every reflection in the room turning at once

## Lore

A **kaleidoscope** is a slowly turning storm of broken mirror fragments, held together by nothing but reflected light. It forms in places where many mirrors have shattered—abandoned halls, collapsed palaces, the workshops of careless enchanters—and it gathers into itself every reflection that was ever lost there.

Looking into a kaleidoscope is unsettling. Each fragment shows a different face: past visitors, long-dead residents, and occasionally the viewer, arriving a few seconds from now. It chimes softly as it turns, like a hall of wind bells, and its light can overwhelm the mind of anyone who stares too long. Blunt force is its weakness; a hammer does what no sword can.

## Signs it is near

- Every mirror in a building shows the same room, from a different angle.
- Faces of the missing appear for a moment in broken glass.
- A sound like a thousand tiny bells, getting closer.

## What characters know

> [!rule] Lore checks
> A character who sees the kaleidoscope can make a check; a higher result also reveals everything below it.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 10:** It is a storm of every reflection ever lost in a place, gathered into one creature.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 15:** It cannot be surprised and sees the invisible; its flash blinds whole groups.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 20:** Bludgeoning damage cracks it; and its shards can show the faces of people the party is searching for.

## In combat

> [!combat] Running the kaleidoscope
> - Refracts to wherever it can catch the most characters in Kaleidoscopic Flash.
> - Lashes anyone who closes in, and reflects missed spell attacks back at the party.
> - Fights to the end in its own hall; outside it, it retreats into the nearest large mirror.
>
> **GM tip.** Let the glass crack loudly on every bludgeoning hit, and use its shards to show faces the party is looking for, a way to deliver clues mid-fight.

## Variants

- **Shattered palace.** A lair: at the end of each round, one mirror in the hall cracks and a glint steps out.
- **Memory kaleidoscope.** Its flash shows a vision instead of Psychic damage: a clue, and the Incapacitated condition until the end of the target's next turn.

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> Its heart is a fist-sized knot of fused mirror glass worth 250 gp, a prized focus for divination. In it, very faintly, every face it ever reflected.

## Hooks

- A collapsed palace's ballroom has been sealed for a century; the sound of bells inside has started again.
- The party's missing ally appears for a moment in a kaleidoscope's shards, mouthing a location.

> [!rumor] What people say
> In the old palace, they say, there is a room where all the broken mirrors sing. Nobody who went to listen came back the same.
