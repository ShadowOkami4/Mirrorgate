# Remnant

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"It remembers being someone. It is the remembering that makes it cruel."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Medium Aberration · CR 2 (450 XP)
> - **Found** Near its own [[Glass Husk]] and [[Glint|glints]], wherever a person broke
> - **Often with** The rest of the same person
> - **Weak to** Its own name and story
> - **Tell** Scraps of an old voice: half a lullaby, a greeting, a name it can't finish

## Lore

A **remnant** is the self that survives when a person is shattered by a mirror: the part that remembers a name, a face, and a life, cut loose from the body and soul that once carried it. It looks like its former self seen through cracked glass—the right shape, but split along a dozen jagged seams that shift when it moves.

Remnants remember being whole, and they cannot forgive anyone who still is. They speak in stolen fragments of their old voice, wear the gestures of their old life, and try to break others the way they were broken—by showing them a reflection they cannot bear to fight.

## Signs it is near

- Mirrors in the area are all turned to face the wall.
- Portraits with the faces scratched out.
- A voice nearby that keeps starting a sentence and never finishing it.

## What characters know

> [!rule] Lore checks
> A character who sees the remnant can make a check; a higher result also reveals everything below it.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 10:** It is the part of a broken person that still remembers being someone, and it hates those who are whole.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 15:** Its touch tears at memory; when it dies, its last shard explodes in confusion.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 20:** Its scraps of speech are its real life; piece them together and you can learn its name.

## In combat

> [!combat] Running the remnant
> - Opens with Mirror of Self against the strongest-looking warrior.
> - Fights beside its husk and glints, and taunts in fragments of its old voice.
> - Its seams glow brighter as it weakens: a warning about the Last Shard.
>
> **GM tip.** Let clever players learn who it used to be from the fragments it speaks.

## Variants

- **Named remnant.** If the party says its true name, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls for 1 round, once per name-speaker.
- **Remnant in a mirror.** It lives in a mirror instead of a body: it can't leave the glass, but it can attack anyone reflected in it.

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> It leaves the item it held onto hardest: a ring, a letter, a toy. Returning it to the right family is often reward enough; sometimes it is worth a quest.

## Hooks

- The party finds a husk, three glints and a remnant that all wear the same ring, and a missing-person poster in the nearest town.
- A remnant keeps humming a lullaby only one living family still knows.

> [!rumor] What people say
> In broken houses there is sometimes a voice that sounds like someone you know, saying half of something. Don't answer it.
