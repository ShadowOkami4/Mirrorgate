# Glint

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"They are drawn to warmth, to voices, and—most unsettlingly—to people who are dying. I prefer to believe they are only lonely."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Small Aberration · CR 1/2 (100 XP)
> - **Found** In swarms near a [[Glass Husk]], around anyone dying
> - **Often with** The husk and [[Remnant]] that were the rest of the same soul
> - **Weak to** Kindness: speaking its name can make it hesitate
> - **Tell** A cold, flickering light at the edge of sight

## Lore

A **glint** is a splinter of a shattered soul: a flickering mote of light about the size of a child, barely aware of itself and painfully lonely. It drifts toward warmth and voices, circling the living as if trying to remember what being one of them was like.

Glints are rarely alone. A shattered soul breaks into several of them, and they tend to stay near the [[Glass Husk]] and [[Remnant]] that were once the rest of the same person. When a glint is destroyed, those nearby sometimes hear a single whispered word—usually a name.

## Signs it is near

- Candles that lean towards a corner where nothing is.
- A wounded companion who says they are not alone.
- Whispers of a single word, usually a name.

## What characters know

> [!rule] Lore checks
> A character who sees the glint can make a check; a higher result also reveals everything below it.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 10:** It is a splinter of a soul, drawn to warmth and voices.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 15:** It hunts the wounded and gathers around anyone at 0 Hit Points; it flashes and blinks away when struck.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 20:** A glint that hears its own name may stop for a moment; one that hears it three times may follow the speaker home.

## In combat

> [!combat] Running the glint
> - Swarms the nearest warm body and darts away with Flicker when hit.
> - Prefers targets that are already hurt, and crowds around anyone who falls.
> - Never fights alone if other glints are near.
>
> **GM tip.** A character who speaks kindly to a glint, or says its name if the party has learned it, may make it hesitate for a round.

## Variants

- **Lantern glint.** Sheds Bright Light in a 10-foot radius; creatures in it have Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
- **Glint swarm.** Hundreds of glints from a whole village, shattered at once: see [[Shardkin#Further ideas]].

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> A destroyed glint leaves a mote of light that fades over an hour. Caught in a bottle, it can serve as a component worth 10 gp for divination spells.

## Hooks

- A healer's patients all report a lonely light at their bedside the night before they recover, or don't.
- A child has been feeding a glint like a pet, and it has started bringing its friends.

> [!rumor] What people say
> Little lights follow the dying in old battlefields and broken houses. Some say they are lost souls; some say they are hungry ones.
