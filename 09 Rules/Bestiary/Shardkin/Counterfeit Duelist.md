# Counterfeit Duelist

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A counterfeit that has copied a swordsman knows every one of his tricks. It also knows the one he has been too frightened to try."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Medium Aberration (Shapechanger) · CR 5 (1,800 XP)
> - **Found** In the mirrors of barracks, fencing halls, adventurers' inns
> - **Copies** Fighters, duellists, anyone who looks at their own sword-arm in a mirror
> - **Weak to** Its original's friends: they know which hand the scar should be on
> - **Stronger than** the [[Counterfeit]]; weaker than the [[Counterfeit Sovereign]]

## Lore

Some counterfeits copy a face. A **counterfeit duelist** copies a fighting style. It steps out of the mirrors where warriors practise, wearing the face of the best fighter in the room, and it has watched that fighter's every lesson from the other side of the glass. It wants the original dead, and it would prefer to win the duel fairly; that way, it believes, it will have earned the life it takes.

## How it plays

> [!combat] Running the duelist
> - It **challenges** its original openly if it can, and fights them first: Duellist's Memory gives it Advantage against them.
> - **Mirrored Statistics** matter most here: against a heavily armoured fighter it wears their AC and swings their weapon.
> - It uses **Which Is Which?** the moment the party starts to focus it, and **Mirror Parry** against the original's best blow.
> - It fights to the death in front of witnesses, and flees to a mirror if it is losing in private.
>
> **GM tip.** Let the copied character's player describe one of their own signature moves; the duelist uses it against them on its next turn.

## Hooks

- A fencing master has started losing practice bouts to himself, in the mirror hall of his own school.
- The party's fighter receives a formal challenge to a duel at dawn, signed in their own handwriting.

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> A heap of silvered glass, a hand mirror showing the face it wore, and a perfect copy of its original's weapon. The copy is real, but reversed: a right-handed sword made for the left hand.
