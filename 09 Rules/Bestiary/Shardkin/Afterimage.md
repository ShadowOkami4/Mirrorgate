# Afterimage

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"I once watched one strike a man who had already walked away. He felt it anyway, two seconds later. He was very cross about it."*

> [!info] At a glance
> - **Kind** Medium Aberration · CR 1 (200 XP)
> - **Found** Where time was damaged: repeating ruins, backward clocks, rooms where something happened too fast
> - **Often with** [[Scar Hazards|war-scars]] and other afterimages from the same moment
> - **Weak to** Moving: step away and its echo misses
> - **Tell** A person-shaped smear of pale light, two seconds late

## Lore

An **afterimage** is a moment that got left behind. It looks like a person-shaped smear of pale light, the kind of shape that lingers in the eye after staring too long at a lamp, and it lives two seconds behind the rest of the world.

Afterimages form where time has been damaged: in ruins where the hours repeat, beside clocks that run backward, and in rooms where something terrible happened too quickly to finish. They endlessly replay the last motions of whoever cast them, and they react violently to anyone who interrupts the replay. Their blows land twice—once when they swing, and again a moment later, when the rest of the world catches up.

## Signs it is near

- Footsteps that sound two seconds after you take them.
- A door that opens, and then opens again.
- A scene replaying itself at the edge of vision: a woman reaching for a door, a guard turning at a noise.

## What characters know

> [!rule] Lore checks
> A character who sees the afterimage can make a check; a higher result also reveals everything below it.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 10:** It is a moment left behind, living two seconds behind the world.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 15:** Its blows land twice; stepping away from where you were hit avoids the echo.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 20:** Left alone it replays its moment; watching the whole replay shows what happened here.

## In combat

> [!combat] Running the afterimage
> - Ignores the party until someone interrupts its replay.
> - Strikes the nearest creature, then moves on; its Out of Step trait makes it slippery.
> - Several afterimages from one moment act together, like the people they were.
>
> **GM tip.** Describe the echo clearly the first time ("you feel the blow start to land a second time") so players learn to step away.

## Variants

- **Procession.** The afterimages of a whole procession: use the night of the ritual in [[SA-01 Chapter 3]].
- **Early image.** An afterimage that runs two seconds *ahead*: it has Advantage on Initiative and attacks where you are about to be.

> [!loot] What it leaves behind
> Nothing physical. A character who watches an afterimage's full replay gains one true clue about what happened here.

## Hooks

- A murder happened in a locked room; the afterimage in it keeps replaying the last two seconds.
- A tavern's patrons have learned to step aside for the afterimage of a waiter who died forty years ago.

> [!rumor] What people say
> In some ruins the past repeats: shapes of light walk the same steps over and over. Leave them be, the old guides say, and they'll leave you be.
