# Shardkin

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A broken mirror does not stop reflecting. It merely becomes less polite about what it shows."*

**Shardkin** are creatures made from broken reflections. Some were once people who met the wrong mirror. Others were never people at all: images, moments or echoes that learned to move without an owner.

> [!info] At a glance
> - **What** Creatures made from broken reflections; eight known kinds, CR 1/2 to 9
> - **Where** Wherever reflection, time or distance has been damaged: cracked mirrors, ruins where the hours run wrong, crossings into [[The Mirrorgate]]
> - **How rare** Very, outside the Mirrorgate. Finding them in the material world means something has gone badly wrong
> - **Not natives** The Mirrorgate has its own plants and animals, and they avoid shardkin as carefully as travellers do (see [[Life in the Mirrorgate]])
> - **Statblocks** All use the **Mirrored Creature** Atlas VTT template (SRD 5.2.1)

## One person, three monsters

- [[Glass Husk]] <small>The body, left behind and held up by glass · CR 1/2</small>
- [[Glint]] <small>The soul, splintered into lonely lights · CR 1/2</small>
- [[Remnant]] <small>The self, jealous of everyone still whole · CR 2</small>

A person shattered by a mirror rarely becomes *one* monster. They break apart: the body stays behind and something jagged moves in; the soul splinters into lights that drift towards anyone warm; and the self, the part that remembers a name and a life, survives and hates. **A husk, a handful of glints and a remnant found together are usually the pieces of the same unfortunate person.**

## Born from reflections

- [[Afterimage]] <small>A moment left behind, two seconds late · CR 1</small>
- [[Counterfeit]] <small>A reflection that wants to replace you · CR 3</small>
- [[Kaleidoscope]] <small>A storm of every reflection lost in a place · CR 6</small>

Counterfeits come in three strengths: the [[Counterfeit]] (CR 3), the [[Counterfeit Duelist]] (CR 5) and the [[Counterfeit Sovereign]] (CR 9 boss). All of them change with the face they wear (*Mirrored Statistics*).

Other shardkin never belonged to anyone. They are what reflections become when the glass breaks and nothing tells them to stop.

## When shardkin are near

> [!shattered] Signs of shardkin
> - Mirrors fog, crack, or show the creature before it appears.
> - Reflections are a moment late, or missing entirely.
> - Voices mimic the last person the creature harmed.
> - Memories leak into the dreams of anyone sleeping nearby.
> - Glittering dust hangs in the air and never settles.

> [!rule] Lore checks
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 10:** shardkin are made from broken reflections, and gather where reflection, time or distance is damaged.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 15:** a husk, glints and a remnant found together were one person; the remnant can be reached through its name.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 20:** mirrors are their doors. Cover or break the mirrors in a room, and counterfeits and remnants lose their way in.

## Running shardkin

> [!combat] At the table
> - **Make them tragic.** They are not beasts; they are what remains when someone is broken by the wrong reflection.
> - **Let the pieces tell a story.** Players who realise a husk, its glints and a remnant were once one person should find a name, a portrait or a diary nearby.
> - **Use the mirrors.** A room with mirrors is more dangerous than one without: counterfeits step out of them, and remnants watch through them.
> - **Foreshadow.** Use the signs above a scene or two before the first fight.

> [!gm]- Shardkin not yet given statblocks
> - **Glint Swarm** — hundreds of glints; what remains when an entire village is shattered at once.
> - **Hour Moths** — pale moths that feed on seconds. Where they swarm, candles burn backward and wounds reopen.
> - **Quicksilver Eel** — lives in still, mirror-bright pools. Its bite steals a creature's reflection, and the reflection swims away.
> - **Frame Warden** — an ornate, empty mirror frame that drifts through a ruin and swallows anyone who looks through it.
> - **Stillborn Moment** — a frozen scene of people caught mid-gesture. It is perfectly harmless until someone touches it.
> - **The Unfinished** — a creature that exists partly in the next moment: its claws strike a round after it swings.

> [!rumor] What people say
> Cover the mirrors when someone dies, the old folk say, and never look into broken glass at night. Something in it might look back, and want to come out.
